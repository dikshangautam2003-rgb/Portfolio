## Why so many REMOVE

Education, Products and Travel were three unrelated content pillars built for a "decision hub" publication model. That model was explicitly abandoned in favour of a personal consulting brand with one focused editorial niche (**Digital Growth in Nepal**). None of those 89 pages had backlink equity or ranking history worth preserving via redirect, so per the locked rule ("a true 404 for genuinely removed content, a permanent redirect only when a real replacement exists") they are removed rather than redirected to an unrelated new page.

If any specific old URL later turns out to have real backlinks or search visibility worth preserving, add a targeted 301 for that one URL in `vercel.json` rather than reintroducing the section.
