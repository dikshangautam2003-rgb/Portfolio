BEFORE YOU SEND

A little context helps.

For a correction: include the page and the specific information that needs attention.

For a suggestion: tell us the decision you’re trying to make. That helps us understand the question behind the topic.

For a business enquiry: be clear about what you’re proposing and who it is for.
