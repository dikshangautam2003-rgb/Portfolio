# Dikshan V7.1 redesign

This pass keeps the V6/V7 URL architecture and existing content inventory intact while refining the visual system and the three pillar pages.

## Pillars
- Education: decision-first gateway with explicit colleges, courses & admissions, study abroad, consultancy, comparison factors and FAQs.
- Products: buying guides, reviews, comparisons, categories and pre-purchase factors.
- Travel: places, treks, things to do, stays and trip-planning factors.

## Visual direction
- Editorial serif display type + restrained sans UI/body.
- Warm paper, navy/blue and restrained red.
- Dividers and whitespace instead of card/shadow/gradient overload.
- Index panels, guide rows and comparison grids create rhythm without looking like SaaS cards.
- Article pages use a narrower reading measure and sticky utility rail on desktop.

## Content rules
- One H1 per page.
- Sentence-case headings.
- Important information is front-loaded.
- No invented rankings, metrics, testing claims or database-size claims.
- Research/methodology is not added to primary navigation.
