Case Study 01

Rebuilding dikshangautam.com.np

SEOContentWeb

The challenge

The site had grown into a 90-page "decision hub" covering Education, Products and Travel — three unrelated industries, each needing its own research, freshness and fact-checking. It looked impressive. It didn't build a business, because it never explained who Dikshan was or why someone should hire him.

My role

Strategy, content architecture, on-page SEO, copywriting and front-end build — end to end, using this project as the proof for the exact process sold on the Services page.

The work

The rebuild followed a locked order: positioning first, then a six-page core sitemap (later expanded to include a curated Education section), then a full URL-by-URL migration decision for the previous 89 pages, then the design system, then the pages themselves.

Every old URL was classified KEEP, REDIRECT or REMOVE — with REMOVE served as a genuine 404 rather than an invented redirect, matching Google's own guidance on handling removed content.

Visually, the site moved from a generic "digital marketing agency" look (gradients, rounded cards, stock photography) to a restrained editorial system: Instrument Serif and Inter, an ivory/ink palette with a single oxblood accent, hairline borders instead of shadows, and a 12-column grid.

The result

A focused six-section-plus-Education site replacing roughly 89 indexable pages, with a documented migration path and a design system built to support real case studies and articles going forward. Traffic and enquiry metrics will be added here once there's enough post-launch data to report honestly.

What I learned

The temptation with a personal site is to keep adding — another content pillar, another service, another page "just in case." The harder and more useful discipline is deciding what to remove.
