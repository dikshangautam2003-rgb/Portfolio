Case Study 01 — Live

This website

Everything you're looking at right now is the first case study. It started as a 90-page content site spanning education, products and travel — a media-publication model that was diluting the actual business. I rebuilt it around one clear positioning, a six-page core site, and a focused editorial niche (Digital Growth in Nepal) instead of three unrelated content pillars.

Work involved: Positioning, sitemap, IA, on-page SEO, content migration, design
Approach

Search-visibility framework

The process I run for a new client: technical health check, keyword and intent mapping, on-page fixes in priority order, then Google Business Profile and local search where relevant. It's the same framework behind the Search system on the Services page.

Used for: Service businesses trying to rank locally or nationally
