## Content URL rules
- Use lowercase, descriptive, stable slugs.
- Keep one canonical URL per substantive content asset.
- Do not create separate pages for trivial keyword variants targeting the same intent.
- Category pages are hubs; articles and profiles sit beneath the most relevant hub.
- Keep commercial disclosures visible on affiliate/sponsored content.
- Article pages should expose published and last-researched dates where applicable.

## Migration principles
Existing legacy pages must be mapped to the closest relevant new destination. Where no useful replacement exists, use an appropriate permanent redirect or retire the URL deliberately; never create irrelevant redirect chains.

## Future article patterns

- Travel guides: /travel/places/places-to-visit-nepal/

- Comparisons: /products/comparisons/product-a-vs-product-b/

## Page responsibilities
Homepage: brand promise, major categories, latest/high-value research, methodology trust signal.
Category: topic overview, featured research, subcategories, internal-link hub.
Article: answer first, decision support, evidence, limitations, methodology, sources, related research.
Profile/review: facts, verdict, best for, limitations, evidence confidence, last researched.
Research: explain methodology and editorial independence.
About: human authorship, mission, accountability.
