Editorial policy

Independence is part of the product.

The point of a recommendation platform is not to sound certain. It is to help readers make a better decision.

Commercial relationships

Coverage can be commercial. Conclusions cannot.

Businesses may approach Dikshan for coverage, featured placements, partnerships or other commercial opportunities. Payment does not guarantee a positive review, ranking or recommendation.

Affiliate links may generate a commission. When used, they are disclosed near the relevant content.

Business response

We make room for corrections and responses.

If a business disputes a factual claim, we consider the evidence and update the page when the evidence supports a correction. A disagreement alone does not determine the editorial outcome.
