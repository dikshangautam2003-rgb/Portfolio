About

Curious about how things work.

I’m Dikshan Gautam, a digital marketing and SEO professional who likes the space where strategy meets execution.

What I do

I work across SEO, content, digital marketing and web development. In practice, that means researching search demand, shaping content, improving pages, reading performance data and building digital experiences when the work needs a technical hand.

I’m particularly interested in making the pieces work together. A keyword is not a strategy. A blog post is not a content system. Traffic is not automatically growth. The useful work happens in the connections between them.

How I think

Start with the audience. Understand the question, problem or desire before choosing a channel.

Use evidence. Search data, analytics, competitors and real performance are more useful than assumptions.

Make it clear. Good marketing should be understandable. Good interfaces should be obvious. Good content should respect the reader’s time.

Keep learning. Search changes, platforms change and technology changes. The underlying habit is to keep asking better questions.

A little track record

My current work includes SEO strategy, content writing, keyword research, analytics and digital marketing at KIEC Education. In the period tracked, organic impressions increased by approximately 60%, while average ranking improved from 14 to 7.

Earlier, I worked in customer support operations, including supervising a small team. That experience still influences how I work: listen carefully, communicate clearly and solve the actual problem.
