Editorial standards for Nepal-focused research

Research and recommendations are separated from commercial relationships. Where a page includes a ranking, review or comparison, the criteria and relevant disclosures are stated clearly.

Articles use a clear topic, useful search context, author information, dates, relevant internal links, featured imagery and structured data where appropriate.
