# Dikshan V10.1 visual polish

This build keeps the V10 content and URL architecture and adds a final visual polish layer:
- blue/red heading hierarchy with controlled typography
- cleaner white/light-blue section rhythm
- premium navigation and hover states
- refined homepage hero and decision selector
- stronger Travel visual hierarchy
- destination-led Places page with real Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan and trekking photography
- improved article and pillar-page consistency
- Blog and Search removed from the primary/mobile navigation while remaining accessible elsewhere
- responsive desktop/mobile refinements

No content routes were intentionally removed.
