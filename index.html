A DIFFERENT KIND OF GUIDE

There is rarely one “best” option.

We don't think a longer list automatically makes a better answer. A useful guide should show the options, explain the meaningful differences and make the trade-offs visible.

Why we work this way →

02Explain the trade-offs.

03Leave room for judgment.
