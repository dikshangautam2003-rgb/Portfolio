Frequently Asked Questions About SEO in Nepal

Why should a business in Nepal invest in organic SEO rather than just social ads? +

Facebook and TikTok ads stop producing leads the second your daily budget pauses. Search Engine Optimization captures people who are already actively seeking your specific service at their moment of peak intent. Once you earn top rankings on Google, those organic clicks continue delivering inquiries without ongoing media cost.

What is included in a typical SEO sprint with Dikshan Gautam? +

Every sprint includes a comprehensive technical audit (crawl errors, indexation, Core Web Vitals), keyword gap analysis against local competitors, Google Business Profile optimization, schema markup integration, and on-page content optimization designed to convert visitors into inquiries.

How do we track progress and know the SEO work is succeeding? +

I provide transparent monthly reporting directly from Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4. You will see exact impressions, clicks, average position changes for target search terms, and verified conversion metrics without confusing agency vanity reports.

Can you work with businesses outside of Kathmandu and Nepal? +

Yes. While I am based in Kathmandu, I regularly collaborate with overseas companies, international education providers, and remote tech teams across Australia, the UK, the US, and Asia.
