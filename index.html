06 · About

I work where search, content and the web meet.

I am Dikshan Gautam. My work sits between SEO, content and websites because those things are rarely separate in the real world. A page can be beautifully designed and still be invisible. A piece of content can rank and still fail to help anyone. A website can look modern and still make the next step difficult.

The goal is simpler: make the right information easier to discover, understand and act on.
