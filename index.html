Why the approach matters

Search visibility is connected to the website and the business process behind it.

SEO can bring a visitor to a page, but the page still needs to answer the question, establish relevance and provide an appropriate next step. Web development should therefore support both usability and search. Content should be planned around real questions and business priorities rather than published only to increase page count.

This approach is designed for businesses that want a practical digital foundation they can maintain and expand over time.
