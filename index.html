A DIFFERENT KIND OF GUIDE

There is rarely one “best” option.

We do not think a longer list automatically makes a better answer. A useful guide should show the options, explain the meaningful differences and make the limits clear.

01Compare the options.

Bring the choices together instead of making you open ten tabs.

02Explain the trade-offs.

More features, lower price or better location can each matter differently.

03Leave the final choice to you.

The goal is clearer thinking, not a universal answer.
