SEO, Web Development & Growth Services in Nepal

Practical digital solutions designed for businesses that need better search visibility, high-performance websites, and structured online growth.

01 / SEARCH

SEO and AEO services in Nepal

Search engine optimization (SEO) improves a website's ability to be discovered and understood in organic search. Answer engine optimization (AEO) focuses on structuring information so search engines and answer systems can identify clear, useful answers.

This service is suitable for businesses that want to improve Google visibility for commercial, local or informational searches. The work can include technical SEO, keyword research, search intent analysis, on-page optimization, internal linking, schema markup, content briefs and page-level improvements.

How the SEO process works

  1. Review the website, competitors, existing rankings and business goals.
  2. Map priority keywords to search intent and the pages that should target them.
  3. Fix technical and structural issues that limit crawling, indexing or usability.
  4. Improve page content, headings, internal links, metadata and structured information.
  5. Measure impressions, clicks, rankings and qualified actions, then refine the plan.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Web development services in Nepal

Web development is the process of building and maintaining a website's structure, interface and functionality. For a business website, development also affects speed, accessibility, content management, search visibility and conversion paths.

The focus is on responsive websites that are easy to navigate and ready for ongoing SEO and content publishing. Projects can include business websites, portfolio sites, landing pages, content systems and WordPress-based implementations.

How the website process works

  1. Define the audience, business goals, required pages and technical requirements.
  2. Create the information architecture, navigation and content structure.
  3. Build responsive front-end components with semantic HTML and accessible interactions.
  4. Prepare technical SEO foundations, metadata, performance and mobile behavior.
  5. Test links, forms, layouts and core user journeys before launch.

03 / GROWTH

Digital marketing and growth services in Nepal

Digital growth connects search, content, website experience and measurement to business objectives. Instead of treating channels as separate activities, the work identifies where digital visibility can create useful traffic, enquiries or other measurable outcomes.

Depending on the project, this can include SEO-led growth planning, content systems, analytics review, conversion-path analysis, competitor research and digital opportunity research for the Nepal market.

How the growth process works

  1. Identify the business objective and the audience that matters.
  2. Review current traffic sources, content, competitors and conversion paths.
  3. Prioritize search, content, website and measurement opportunities.
  4. Build an execution roadmap with clear owners, priorities and metrics.
  5. Review performance and use the evidence to improve the next cycle.
