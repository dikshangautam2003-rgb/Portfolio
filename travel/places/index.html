Where should you go? Compare Nepal destinations by trip fit

Compare Nepal destinations by the kind of trip you want, the time you have and the conditions you are comfortable with. Start with a place, then use the deeper guides to make the shortlist smaller.

This page is for choosing a destination.

Use it before you plan the itinerary. The useful question is not only “What is popular?” but “Which place fits this trip?”

Destination guides

Three places, three different trips.

These are the dedicated destination guides currently available in Dikshan's Places section. Each one goes deeper than a photo list.

01 · Kathmandu

For heritage, culture and a city-based trip.

Kathmandu combines historic architecture, religious sites and a busy urban setting. Nepal Tourism Board highlights Kathmandu Durbar Square, Swayambhunath, Boudha, Pashupatinath, Thamel and Asan among places to see.

Best forHeritage, culture & city exploration
Trip feelUrban, active, culturally focused
TimingConsider warmer May–July conditions
Kathmandu guideThings to do

02 · Pokhara

For lake views, mountain scenery and a more relaxed base.

Pokhara is known for views of the Annapurna range, Phewa Lake and a strong mix of relaxation and adventure activities. The Nepal Tourism Board also lists boating, birdwatching, trekking, mountain biking and paragliding among its attractions.

Best forLake, mountain views & adventure
Trip feelSlower pace with outdoor options
TimingConsider warmer May–July conditions
Pokhara guideTreks

03 · Chitwan

For wildlife, nature and a lowland change of pace.

Chitwan offers jungle and wildlife experiences alongside Tharu culture. Nepal Tourism Board lists jungle safaris, birdwatching, canoe rides, jungle walks and visits around Sauraha and the national park among the area's options.

Best forWildlife, nature & local culture
Trip feelOutdoor, warm, experience-led
TimingOct–Mar is the stated best season

Season changes the choice

Check the conditions before you lock the destination.

Nepal Tourism Board notes that Kathmandu and Pokhara sit in the mid-hills, while Chitwan is in the Terai. The seasonal difference matters: Kathmandu and Pokhara are cooler outside the warmer May–July period, while Chitwan is hot in March–July, rainy in July–September and listed by NTB as best in October–March.

For trekking, NTB recommends September–November and February–May as the better walking periods in its general travel guidance.
