QUESTIONS TRAVELERS ASK

Travel in Nepal: FAQs

What are the best places to visit in Nepal?

The answer depends on what you want from the trip. Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan offer very different experiences, so choosing based on your interests and available time is often more useful than looking for one universally “best” destination.

How do I choose where to go in Nepal?

Consider how much time you have, your budget, the season, travel time and the type of experience you want.

How many days do I need for a Nepal trip?

There is no single ideal duration. A shorter trip can work well when you focus on fewer places rather than trying to cover too much.

How do I choose a trek in Nepal?

Compare difficulty, duration, scenery, season, logistics and the level of trekking experience required.

Should I book a hotel based only on its rating?

No. Location, access, facilities and how well the property fits your particular trip can be just as important.
