PLAN THE TRIP

Good travel planning is not about filling every hour.

It is about understanding the decisions that can affect the experience, then leaving enough flexibility for the trip itself.

FactorQuestion to ask
TimeHow many days do you actually have?
SeasonWhat will conditions be like when you travel?
Travel timeHow much of the trip will be spent getting from place to place?
ExperienceWhat kind of experience are you looking for?
BudgetWhat level of spending makes sense for this trip?
PracticalityWhat logistics, access or preparation could affect the plan?
