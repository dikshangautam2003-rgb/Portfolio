NARROW IT DOWN

What matters for your trip?

Two destinations can look similar on a list and feel very different in practice. Use these questions before you choose.

01

Destination

Do you want culture and city life, lakes and mountain views, wildlife, or a quieter base?

02

Trip type

Is this a short break, family trip, couple's trip, outdoor adventure, cultural visit or trek?

03

Season

Will the month you travel change the weather, trail conditions, crowds or what you can do?

04

Time

How many days can you give the trip, including the time spent travelling between places?

05

Budget

What can you spend on transport, stays, food, activities and permits without stretching the trip?

06

Experience

How much walking, altitude, planning and physical effort are you comfortable with?

DESTINATIONS

Three very different ways to experience Nepal.

Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan are useful starting points because they solve different travel needs. Compare the character of the trip before you build an itinerary.

01 · KATHMANDU

Culture, heritage and city life.

Start here if temples, historic neighbourhoods, food and a busy urban base are part of the trip.

02 · POKHARA

Lakes, mountain views and easy access to outdoor experiences.

A natural fit when scenery and a slower lakeside base matter.

03 · CHITWAN

Wildlife and a very different side of Nepal.

Consider it when jungle, wildlife and the Terai landscape are the reason for the trip.
