About

Hi, I'm Dikshan.

I work at the intersection of search, content and the web.

This site used to try to be three businesses at once — an education resource, a product-comparison site and a travel guide — while barely explaining what I actually do. I rebuilt it around one question: how does someone find a business, and what do they see when they do? Search, content and web all sit under that question.

How I Work

Fewer things, done properly.

I don't sell a long menu of freelancer skills. Search, content and web are one connected system; video sits inside content rather than being sold on its own. Education is a separate, editorial part of the site — practical guides for Nepali students, kept deliberately small and useful rather than another sprawling publication.

A Local Advantage

I understand how Nepal actually searches.

Search behaviour here mixes English, Nepali and Romanized Nepali, often in the same session — and local intent frequently matters more than raw search volume. That shapes how I approach keyword research and content for Nepali businesses.
