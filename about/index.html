About Dikshan

A better place to figure things out in Nepal.

Dikshan is being built around a simple idea: useful information should make decisions easier, not make people do more homework.

What we are building

Dikshan brings together practical guides, comparisons and recommendations across education, products and travel. The goal is not to cover everything. It is to become genuinely useful for the decisions we do cover.

Who it is for

Students choosing a college. People deciding what to buy. Travelers planning a Nepal trip. Anyone who wants the useful differences explained clearly before making a choice.

How the platform grows

We start with questions people already have, build the pages around those decisions, and improve them as better information becomes available.
