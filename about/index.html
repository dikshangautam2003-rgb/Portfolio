About

About Dikshan

Dikshan is a Nepal-focused research and recommendation platform built around a simple idea: do the homework behind everyday decisions and make the result easier to use.

What Dikshan is building

Dikshan brings research, discovery, comparison and recommendation into one place for people in Nepal.

The initial focus is Education, Products and Travel. Each is treated as a product area rather than a generic blog category.

The long-term goal is simple: make useful decision support easier to find, understand and trust.

The promise

Deep research underneath. Simple, honest advice on the surface.

The test

A page is successful when a visitor can leave thinking: “I found exactly what I needed.”
