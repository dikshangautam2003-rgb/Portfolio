How I think about the work

I care about the part between being found and being chosen.

That means looking at the whole path: what someone is trying to understand, how they find the answer, whether the page keeps its promise, and what happens after they arrive.

I work independently, which keeps the process close to the actual problem. The goal is not to make the website sound impressive. It is to make the useful thing easier to discover, understand and act on.
