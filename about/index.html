About Me

Building smarter digital paths for Nepal through SEO and web engineering.

Dikshan Gautam is a Kathmandu-based digital professional specializing in search engine optimization, web architecture, and performance-driven digital growth strategies tailored for the local market.

Who

A digital professional focused on search, websites and practical growth systems.

The work sits between marketing and implementation: understanding what people search for, structuring information clearly, building useful web experiences and using performance data to improve the next decision.

The Nepal focus matters because search behavior, business needs, local services and digital adoption vary by market. Content and recommendations should therefore be grounded in the actual context rather than copied from generic international advice.

What

Core areas of work

Three service areas are supported by a long-term research and publishing system.

01 / SEARCH

SEO & AEO

Technical SEO, keyword and search intent research, information architecture, internal linking, content optimization and answer-focused structure.

02 / WEB

Web Development

Responsive websites, landing pages, WordPress implementations and content structures designed for usability, performance and search.

03 / GROWTH

Digital Growth

Search-led content planning, analytics review, digital research and practical growth roadmaps.

Who

Research, build, measure and improve.

The process begins with the problem and the intended audience. Research identifies demand, competitors and the information users need. Implementation turns that research into pages, systems or content. Measurement then shows what is working and what needs to change.

The same principle applies to the Insights section: research first, clear evidence, transparent methodology and useful recommendations.
