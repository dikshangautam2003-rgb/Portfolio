About Dikshan

A small research desk for better decisions.

Dikshan is being built to answer a simple question: what would you want to know if you had to make this decision yourself?

The mission

Do the homework. Share the useful part.

Dikshan started from digital research and SEO work, but the long-term goal is bigger: build a trusted Nepal-focused resource where recommendations are backed by visible reasoning.

The public-facing advice should stay simple. The research underneath should not.

Founder

Dikshan Gautam

Founder and editor of Dikshan. The editorial role is to make the final call, challenge weak evidence and keep commercial incentives separate from conclusions.
