Community & Purpose

The Student Education Desk Initiative

Why I maintain free educational guides for Nepali students across +2, Bachelor's, and Study Abroad transitions.

Every year, tens of thousands of young Nepali students complete their SEE and +2 examinations and face aggressive marketing from private colleges and consultancies. Clear, objective guidance on curriculum, hidden fee structures, accreditation, and scholarship quotas is nearly impossible to find.

The Education Desk is my commitment to public, plain-language publishing. By applying professional search and content architecture, I ensure students and their families can easily discover reliable answers when making the most important educational choices of their lives.
