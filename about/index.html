SEO Specialist in Nepal Helping Businesses Rank Higher on Google

I work across SEO, websites and content to help businesses improve visibility, communicate clearly and turn search traffic into useful action.

The approach

SEO should lead somewhere useful.

Too many SEO campaigns stop at an impression. They drive traffic to confusing pages that fail to answer the visitor’s core question.

My work looks at the full journey: the problem someone is trying to solve, the query they type, whether the page keeps its promise, and what should happen next.

How I work
01

Search data

Search Console, analytics and competitor research to understand what people are actually looking for and where the opportunity sits.

02

Technical foundation

Indexing, crawlability, structure, page experience, internal links and metadata are cleaned up before adding more noise.

03

Useful content

Pages and articles are written around intent, clarity and the next question a real customer is likely to ask.

04

Iteration

Use search performance and user behaviour to decide what to improve next instead of publishing on autopilot.
