ABOUT

Helping people make clearer decisions in Nepal.

A Nepal-focused guide and comparison platform covering education, products and travel. We bring useful options together, explain the differences that matter and make practical information easier to use.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Information isn’t the problem. Knowing what to do with it is.

You can find hundreds of pages about a college, a product or a destination. The harder part is working out which information matters, what the alternatives are and how the differences affect your decision.

We organize information around the decisions people actually face. Instead of simply collecting options, we try to explain what makes them different, where each option makes sense and what you should check before deciding.

The goal is straightforward: make the next decision easier.

OUR APPROACH

How we approach a decision.

We don’t think every question has one universal answer.

01

Compare

Bring the relevant options together.

02

Explain

Focus on differences that can actually affect the decision.

03

04

Decide

Leave the final choice with the person making it.

WHAT WE DON’T DO

Useful advice starts with knowing its limits.

01

No universal “best.”

The right choice depends on the person, situation and priorities.

02

No specs without context.

More features don’t automatically mean better value.

03

No recommendations without reasons.

If something is recommended, the useful part is understanding why.

04

No decisions made for you.

We narrow the field and make the trade-offs clearer. Your decision stays yours.

TRUST & EDITORIAL

Good information should be easier to question.

We aim to explain the reasoning behind useful comparisons and point out what readers should verify before acting on information. The site is built around clarity rather than a single universal answer.
