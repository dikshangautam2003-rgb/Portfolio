About

A research and recommendation platform for decisions in Nepal.

We bring useful comparisons and practical guidance into one place.

Why Dikshan exists

Finding information is easy. Working out what it means for your decision is harder.

Dikshan is built around that gap. We bring relevant options together, explain meaningful differences and make practical details easier to understand.

Three places to start

Education helps with colleges, courses, admissions, study abroad and consultancies. Products helps with buying guides, reviews and comparisons. Travel helps with destinations, activities, treks and stays.

Useful first

The goal is not to make a page look impressive. It is to make the next decision easier.
