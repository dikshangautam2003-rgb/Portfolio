About Dikshan

A small research desk for better decisions.

Dikshan is being built around a simple question: what would you want to know if you had to make this decision yourself?

The mission

Do the homework. Share the useful part.

Dikshan is a Nepal-focused research and recommendation platform. The public-facing advice should stay simple. The research underneath should not.

Education builds authority. Products create useful commercial pathways. Travel expands the search footprint. Across all three, the standard is the same: evidence before confidence.

Founder & editor

Dikshan Gautam

The editorial role is to make the final call, challenge weak evidence and keep commercial incentives separate from conclusions.
