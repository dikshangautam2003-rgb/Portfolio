About

Digital marketing, built around one question.

How does someone find this business — and what do they see when they do? Everything I do sits under that question: search, content and web, working together instead of competing for budget.

I'm Dikshan Gautam. I work in SEO, content and digital growth, based in Nepal and working with businesses locally and abroad. I don't pretend every project needs everything — I tell you what actually matters for your situation.

Why I Work This Way

I used to try to do everything. It didn't work.

This site used to be a 90-page publication covering education, products and travel — three unrelated industries, each needing constant research, updates and fact-checking. It looked impressive. It didn't build a business. I rebuilt it — and the way I think about client work — around a much simpler idea: pick fewer things, connect them, and do them properly.

A Local Advantage

I understand how Nepal actually searches.

Search behaviour in Nepal isn't purely English — it's a mix of English, Nepali and Romanized Nepali, and local intent often matters more than raw search volume. That's a genuinely useful edge for any business trying to be found by Nepali customers, and it shapes how I approach keyword research and content here.
