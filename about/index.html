The short version

I like work that has a reason to exist.

I started with the instinct to learn everything: SEO, content, websites, editing, marketing. Over time, the useful pattern became clearer. The work connects.

Search tells you what people are asking. Content gives them something worth finding. A website turns that attention into understanding and action.

Alongside client work, I’m building an education resource for Nepali students because the same principle applies there: clear information makes difficult decisions easier.
