# Dikshan — URL migration map

The new site is a product-first research platform. Legacy portfolio/service routes are not reproduced as indexable pages.

| Legacy URL | New destination | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/services/` | `/about/` | 301 | The old service positioning is no longer a primary product area. |
| `/work/` | `/research/` | 301 | Research is the closest new editorial/product destination. |
| `/insights/` | `/research/` | 301 | Legacy insights are consolidated into the research hub. |
| `/insights/seo-digital-growth/` | `/research/` | 301 | Old SEO editorial content no longer matches a standalone product category. |
| `/insights/web-technology/` | `/products/` | 301 | Broad technology/product intent is closer to the Products desk. |
| `/insights/nepal-services-business/` | `/education/` | 301 | Legacy service/business content is not being recreated as a standalone desk. |
| `/insights/nepal-guides/` | `/travel/` | 301 | Nepal guide intent is consolidated under Travel where relevant. |
| `/insights/search-intent/` | `/research/methodology/` | 301 | Research/methodology is the closest surviving destination. |
| `/insights/content-system/` | `/research/methodology/` | 301 | Research process is consolidated under methodology. |
| `/insights/seo-page/` | `/research/methodology/` | 301 | Old page-optimization content is not a new product category. |

Before launch, inspect Search Console data and server logs. If a legacy URL has meaningful backlinks or traffic, consider a more specific destination rather than accepting a broad redirect.
