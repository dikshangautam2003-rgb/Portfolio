# +2 Science in Nepal: Subjects, Course Structure and Career Pathways

Choosing the right subjects after SEE is an important decision for students planning their higher education. For students interested in medicine, engineering, information technology, research or scientific studies, **+2 Science in Nepal** is one of the most popular academic choices.

Commonly known as Plus Two Science, this programme covers Grades 11 and 12 under the National Examinations Board (NEB). Students develop their understanding of scientific concepts through subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and Computer Science, alongside compulsory subjects.

However, choosing Science is not simply about selecting a programme. Students also need to understand the available subject combinations, their academic interests and the university courses they may want to pursue after Grade 12.

This guide explains the +2 Science course in Nepal, including its subjects, course structure, eligibility, examination system, colleges and future academic and career pathways.

## What Is +2 Science in Nepal?

+2 Science is a two-year higher secondary academic programme for students who have completed the Secondary Education Examination (SEE) or an equivalent qualification.

The programme focuses on developing scientific knowledge, mathematical understanding, analytical thinking and practical learning skills. It prepares students for undergraduate education in scientific, medical, engineering, technological and other academic disciplines.

The programme is regulated by the National Examinations Board (NEB), which is responsible for conducting the Grade 12 national examination.

Students commonly refer to this programme as the Science stream. However, Nepal's revised Grade 11 and 12 curriculum uses compulsory and elective subject categories rather than relying entirely on the older fixed-stream structure. Colleges may still use familiar labels such as Science, Management and Humanities when presenting their programmes.

## Why Choose +2 Science After SEE?

Students choose +2 Science for different academic and professional reasons. Some are interested in becoming doctors or engineers, while others want to explore technology, research, environmental science or other scientific disciplines.

The programme provides a foundation for a wide range of undergraduate courses.

### 1. Opportunities in Medical and Health Sciences

Students interested in healthcare and medical professions can select relevant science subjects, particularly Biology and Chemistry, alongside the other subjects required for their intended programmes.

Possible future academic options include MBBS, BDS, BSc Nursing, Bachelor of Pharmacy, public health and other health-science programmes. Each programme has its own subject, grade and entrance requirements.

### 2. Opportunities in Engineering

Students interested in mathematics, technology, construction, machines and problem-solving can prepare for engineering studies.

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics are particularly important for many engineering entrance and admission pathways.

Possible fields include civil engineering, computer engineering, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering and electronics and communication engineering.

### 3. Opportunities in Information Technology

Students interested in computers, software, programming and digital technology can explore IT-related undergraduate programmes.

Depending on the programme's eligibility requirements, possible options include BSc CSIT, BIT, computer engineering and other computing-related degrees.

### 4. Opportunities in Research and Natural Sciences

Students who enjoy scientific experiments, investigation and understanding the natural world can continue into science-related degrees.

Potential fields include Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Biotechnology, Environmental Science and Microbiology.

### 5. Opportunities Beyond Traditional Science Careers

Studying Science does not mean students must pursue only medical or engineering careers. Depending on university admission requirements, Science graduates can also explore business, management, economics, social sciences and other academic disciplines.

## Subjects in +2 Science in Nepal

The subjects offered in +2 Science generally include Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and Computer Science, along with compulsory subjects.

The exact subject combination depends on the curriculum, available electives and the college's approved offerings.

### Compulsory Subjects

English and Nepali are among the compulsory subjects in the Grade 11 and Grade 12 curriculum. Other compulsory subject requirements and their placement differ between the two grades under the national curriculum.

Students should check the current NEB subject structure and their college's subject-selection information before admission.

### Major Science Subjects

**1. Physics**

Physics focuses on matter, energy, motion, forces and the fundamental principles governing the physical world.

Major areas of study include:

- Mechanics
- Heat and thermodynamics
- Waves and sound
- Electricity and magnetism
- Optics
- Modern physics

Physics is particularly relevant for students interested in engineering, physical sciences and technology.

**2. Chemistry**

Chemistry studies the composition, properties and transformations of matter. It helps students understand how substances interact and how chemical reactions occur.

Major areas include:

- Atomic structure
- Chemical bonding
- States of matter
- Chemical reactions
- Organic chemistry
- Inorganic chemistry
- Physical chemistry

Chemistry is important for students considering medicine, pharmacy, biotechnology and other science-related degrees.

**3. Biology**

Biology focuses on living organisms, their structures, functions, interactions and development.

Major areas include:

- Cell biology
- Genetics
- Plant biology
- Human and animal physiology
- Evolution
- Ecology
- Biodiversity

Biology is particularly relevant to students interested in medical science, healthcare, agriculture, biotechnology and life sciences.

**4. Mathematics**

Mathematics develops logical reasoning, analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Major areas may include:

- Algebra
- Trigonometry
- Calculus
- Coordinate geometry
- Statistics
- Probability
- Vectors

Mathematics is an important subject for engineering, computer science, physical sciences and other quantitative disciplines.

**5. Computer Science**

Computer Science introduces students to computing concepts, programming and the use of technology to solve problems.

Depending on the curriculum, topics may include:

- Fundamentals of computing
- Programming concepts
- Computer systems
- Data representation
- Database concepts
- Computer networks
- Information technology

It is useful for students interested in software development, computing, IT and related fields. However, taking Computer Science at +2 does not automatically replace Mathematics where a university programme specifically requires Mathematics.

### Can Students Study Both Biology and Mathematics?

Yes, some colleges offer subject combinations that include both Biology and Mathematics. This can provide students with flexibility when they are interested in both medical and mathematical or scientific pathways.

However, students should not assume every college offers this combination. They should confirm the available electives, timetable and subject-registration rules before admission.

## Practical Learning and Assessment in +2 Science

Science education involves more than classroom lectures and textbook learning. Students are also expected to develop practical understanding through laboratory activities, experiments and subject-based assignments.

Depending on the subject, practical learning may involve:

- Physics experiments and measurements
- Chemical reactions and laboratory procedures
- Biological observations and experiments
- Computer-based practical exercises
- Assignments, projects and presentations

Assessment generally includes theoretical examinations and internal or practical assessment where applicable. The exact assessment structure and marks distribution depend on the subject and current NEB examination guidelines.

## Eligibility for +2 Science in Nepal

Students who have successfully completed SEE or an equivalent qualification can apply for Grade 11 Science programmes.

However, admission requirements can differ depending on the college, its admission policy and the applicable NEB guidelines.

Some colleges may consider students' SEE GPA, grades in Mathematics and Science, entrance examination performance or other admission criteria.

Before applying, students should:

1. Complete SEE or an equivalent qualification.
2. Check the latest NEB eligibility requirements.
3. Review the college's minimum GPA and subject-grade requirements.
4. Confirm the available Science subject combinations.
5. Complete the college's admission and registration procedures.

Students should always verify the latest admission notice rather than relying on previous years' GPA requirements.

## Admission Process for +2 Science

The admission process generally begins after the publication of SEE results.

Although individual colleges may follow different procedures, the common steps include:

1. **Choose a college:** Research NEB-affiliated institutions offering Grade 11 Science.
2. **Check admission requirements:** Review GPA, subject-grade and entrance examination requirements.
3. **Submit an application:** Complete the college's admission form and provide the required documents.
4. **Attend an entrance examination or interview:** Complete these if required by the institution.
5. **Select subjects:** Choose an available combination based on academic interests and future study plans.
6. **Complete admission:** Submit the necessary documents and fees to confirm enrolment.

## +2 Science Fees in Nepal

The cost of studying +2 Science varies between colleges and locations.

Private institutions may have different fee structures depending on their facilities, teaching resources, laboratory arrangements, academic support and other services.

Before admission, students should request a complete fee breakdown covering:

- Admission and registration fees
- Tuition fees
- Annual or term charges
- Laboratory fees
- Examination fees
- Practical and project charges
- Transportation and other optional services

Some institutions also provide merit-based, need-based or other scholarship opportunities.

It is advisable to compare the total two-year cost rather than considering only the initial admission fee.

## Is +2 Science Difficult?

+2 Science can be academically demanding because it involves theoretical concepts, mathematical problem-solving, practical experiments and regular assessments.

However, its difficulty depends on a student's interests, learning habits and preparation.

Students who enjoy understanding how things work, solving numerical problems, conducting experiments and exploring scientific concepts may find the programme engaging.

To manage the workload, students should:

- Build a strong foundation in Mathematics and Science.
- Understand concepts instead of relying only on memorisation.
- Practise numerical problems regularly.
- Attend laboratory sessions and complete practical work.
- Revise lessons consistently.
- Seek help when difficult topics arise.
- Maintain a balanced study routine.

## How to Choose the Right Subjects in +2 Science

Choosing subjects based only on popularity or what friends are studying can create difficulties later.

Students should consider their interests and the admission requirements of the undergraduate courses they may want to pursue.

For example:

- If you are interested in medicine, explore Biology-based combinations and the specific requirements of medical programmes.
- If you want to study engineering, prioritise Mathematics, Physics and the required science subjects.
- If you are interested in software and IT, consider Mathematics and Computer Science while checking the requirements of your target degree.
- If you enjoy scientific research, explore combinations that support your preferred area of science.
- If you are undecided, ask colleges about combinations that provide broader academic flexibility.

**A useful rule:** Decide which undergraduate courses you may want to pursue first, then check their eligibility requirements before finalising your +2 subject combination.

## Frequently Asked Questions About +2 Science in Nepal

### 1. How many years is +2 Science in Nepal?

+2 Science is a two-year academic programme covering Grade 11 and Grade 12 under the National Examinations Board.

### 2. What are the main subjects in +2 Science?

The commonly offered science subjects include Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and Computer Science. Students also study compulsory subjects such as English and Nepali. The exact combination depends on the current curriculum and college offerings.

### 3. Is Biology compulsory in +2 Science?

Biology is not compulsory for every student following a Science-related subject combination. Students may select Biology or other available electives depending on their academic goals and the college's subject offerings.

### 4. Can I study engineering after +2 Science?

Yes. Students who meet the required subject, grade and entrance examination criteria can apply for engineering programmes. Mathematics and Physics are important for many engineering pathways.

### 5. Can I study MBBS after +2 Science?

Yes. Students who meet the required academic and subject eligibility criteria can apply for MBBS programmes through the applicable medical entrance and admission process.

### 6. Can I study BSc CSIT after +2 Science?

Yes. +2 Science students may apply for BSc CSIT if they meet the university's current eligibility and entrance examination requirements.

### 7. Can I study IT after choosing Biology?

It depends on the IT programme and university. Some computing programmes have specific Mathematics or subject requirements. Students should check the admission criteria before selecting their +2 subjects.

### 8. Can Science students study BBA or other management courses?

Yes. Science students may be eligible for several business and management programmes, depending on the university's admission requirements.

### 9. What is the difference between Biology and Physical groups?

The Biology group commonly includes Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology, while the Physical group commonly includes Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Computer Science. These are familiar subject-combination labels used by colleges.

### 10. What is the scope of +2 Science in Nepal?

+2 Science provides a foundation for higher education in medicine, engineering, IT, agriculture, biotechnology, pharmacy, natural sciences and other fields. Professional opportunities generally depend on the undergraduate qualification and any additional licensing or professional requirements.

### 11. Is Computer Science better than Biology in +2 Science?

Neither subject is universally better. Biology is more directly relevant to life sciences and many healthcare pathways, while Computer Science is useful for computing and technology-related interests. The right choice depends on the student's goals and university eligibility requirements.

### 12. Can I change my subject combination after admission?

Subject changes may be possible under certain institutional and NEB registration conditions, but they are not guaranteed. Students should consult their college before making any changes.

## Conclusion

+2 Science in Nepal offers students a foundation for a wide range of academic opportunities in medical science, engineering, information technology, agriculture and natural sciences.

However, choosing the right subject combination is just as important as selecting the programme itself. Students should understand the differences between Biology, Mathematics and Computer Science, explore their interests and check the admission requirements of their intended undergraduate courses.

Before enrolling, compare colleges based on their academic environment, laboratory facilities, subject availability, fees and student support.

A well-informed subject choice after SEE can help students build a clearer academic direction and prepare for their future studies.
