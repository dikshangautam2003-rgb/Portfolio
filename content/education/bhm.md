# BHM in Nepal: Course, Fees, Syllabus and Career Scope

The hospitality and tourism industry offers career opportunities across hotels, resorts, restaurants, airlines, travel businesses, event companies and other service-oriented organisations. For students who enjoy working with people, managing services and developing practical business skills, **Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM)** can be a relevant undergraduate degree.

BHM in Nepal combines academic learning with practical hospitality training. Students study hotel operations, food production, food and beverage service, housekeeping, front office management, accounting, marketing, human resource management and other areas related to the hospitality business.

The programme is not limited to learning how hotels operate. It also develops the management, communication, customer-service and operational skills needed to work in a professional hospitality environment.

This guide explains the BHM course in Nepal, including its duration, eligibility, entrance examination, syllabus, subjects, fees, colleges, career opportunities and future scope.

## What Is BHM?

BHM stands for **Bachelor of Hotel Management**. It is an undergraduate degree that prepares students for professional roles in the hotel, hospitality, food service and tourism industries.

The course covers both the operational and managerial sides of hospitality. Students learn how different hotel departments function, how guest services are delivered, how food and beverages are prepared and served, and how hospitality businesses are managed.

Under Tribhuvan University (TU), the BHM programme follows a four-year semester system consisting of eight semesters and 126 credit hours. Its curriculum includes compulsory management courses, hospitality-focused core subjects, an elective course and industrial exposure.

The exact structure can differ between universities and institutions. Therefore, students should check the curriculum and affiliation of their chosen college before applying.

## Why Study BHM in Nepal?

BHM can be a suitable choice for students who want to build a career in the hospitality industry rather than pursue a degree focused only on theoretical business studies.

Here are some reasons students consider this programme.

### 1. Practical and Industry-Oriented Learning

Hospitality is a service-based industry where practical skills matter. BHM includes learning activities related to food production, food and beverage service, housekeeping and front office operations.

Students get opportunities to understand how hospitality departments function and how service standards are maintained.

### 2. Exposure to Different Hospitality Departments

The programme introduces students to several areas of hotel operations. This allows them to understand different career paths before choosing a particular specialisation.

Students can explore areas such as:

- Front office and guest relations
- Food production and culinary operations
- Food and beverage service
- Housekeeping and room division
- Hotel administration
- Event and hospitality management

### 3. Management and Business Knowledge

BHM also includes business-related subjects such as accounting, economics, marketing, financial management, entrepreneurship and human resource management.

These subjects help students understand the commercial and managerial responsibilities involved in running hospitality businesses.

### 4. Industrial Exposure and Internship

Practical industry experience is an important part of the TU BHM curriculum. Students complete industrial exposure during the final two semesters, connecting their academic learning with professional hospitality environments.

This experience can help students understand workplace expectations, operational procedures and professional responsibilities.

### 5. Opportunities in Nepal and Abroad

Hospitality businesses operate across international markets. Graduates may explore opportunities in hotels, resorts, restaurants, cruise-related services, tourism businesses and other hospitality organisations, depending on their experience, skills and employer requirements.

## BHM Eligibility Criteria in Nepal

Students who have completed their higher secondary education can apply for BHM, provided they meet the admission requirements of the university or institution offering the programme.

For the Tribhuvan University Faculty of Management BHM programme, the published curriculum specifies the following academic requirements:

- Completion of twelve years of schooling or an equivalent qualification recognised by Tribhuvan University.
- Minimum D grade in each subject of Grades 11 and 12 with a CGPA of 1.80 or above, under the applicable grading provision.
- For candidates covered by the earlier grading provision, the curriculum also mentions a minimum D+ grade in each subject with a CGPA of 1.80 or above.
- Alternatively, candidates with at least second division in 10+2, PCL or an equivalent programme may qualify.
- Applicants must complete the prescribed application and admission procedures.

Eligibility requirements can be updated through university admission notices. Students should confirm the applicable criteria for their admission year before applying.

### Can Management Students Study BHM?

Yes. Students from management backgrounds can apply if they meet the relevant university eligibility requirements.

### Can Science and Humanities Students Study BHM?

BHM is not limited to students from the management stream. Applicants from other academic streams may also be eligible, subject to the university's published admission criteria.

## BHM Entrance Exam in Nepal

Students applying for the TU Faculty of Management BHM programme are required to appear in the **Central Management Admission Test (CMAT)**.

The CMAT assesses applicants in four main areas:

1. Verbal Ability
2. Quantitative Ability
3. Logical Reasoning
4. General Awareness

The TU BHM curriculum specifies a 100-mark objective entrance test, with 25 questions from each section. Candidates must secure at least 40% in the CMAT to qualify for the interview stage.

Shortlisted candidates are interviewed as part of the admission process. Final selection is based on the university's admission procedure and applicable notice.

Students applying to BHM programmes under other universities should check their respective entrance examination and selection requirements. CMAT should not be assumed to apply to every BHM programme in Nepal.

## Practical Learning in BHM

Practical learning is one of the defining features of hotel management education. Unlike programmes that rely primarily on classroom instruction, BHM includes activities designed to help students understand the actual work carried out in hospitality organisations.

Depending on the college and curriculum, practical learning may include:

### Food Production and Culinary Practice

Students learn food preparation, kitchen procedures, cooking methods, ingredient handling, food safety and basic bakery or pastry techniques.

### Food and Beverage Service

This area focuses on restaurant operations, table service, beverage knowledge, guest interaction and service standards.

### Housekeeping Operations

Students learn room preparation, cleaning procedures, linen management, maintenance coordination and housekeeping standards.

### Front Office Operations

This area introduces reception duties, reservations, guest registration, check-in and check-out procedures and guest relations.

### Industrial Exposure

Students gain experience in a professional hospitality environment, where they can observe and participate in operational activities under the organisation's supervision.

## Scope of BHM in Nepal

The scope of BHM is closely connected to the hospitality and tourism industry. Graduates can explore employment opportunities in hotels, resorts, restaurants, catering businesses, event management companies and other organisations that require hospitality and service management skills.

The degree provides an academic foundation for understanding hotel operations and hospitality businesses. However, career progression also depends on practical experience, communication skills, professional performance, specialisation and the requirements of individual employers.

### 1. Hotel and Resort Management

Hotels and resorts require professionals to coordinate daily operations, manage departments, maintain service standards and handle guest requirements.

BHM graduates may begin in operational or supervisory roles and progress towards departmental or general management positions with experience.

Graduates interested in this area can build careers in food and beverage operations.

Front office professionals handle reservations, guest registration, check-in and check-out procedures, guest enquiries and coordination with other departments.

This area can suit students with strong communication and customer-service skills.

### 4. Culinary and Food Production

Students interested in cooking, kitchen operations and food production can explore opportunities in hotel kitchens, restaurants, catering services and other food businesses.

Professional development and specialised culinary training can support career progression in this area.

### 5. Housekeeping and Accommodation Management

Housekeeping departments maintain the cleanliness, organisation and presentation of hotel rooms and public spaces.

Graduates can pursue operational and supervisory roles in housekeeping and accommodation services.

### 6. Event and Banquet Management

Hotels and event companies organise conferences, weddings, meetings, celebrations and other functions.

BHM graduates may explore event coordination, banquet operations and hospitality event management.

### 7. Tourism and Related Services

Hospitality knowledge can also be relevant to tourism businesses, travel-related services and organisations that coordinate accommodation and guest experiences.

## Is BHM a Good Course in Nepal?

BHM can be a suitable course for students who are interested in hospitality, hotel operations, food service, tourism and customer-facing business environments.

Before choosing the course, students should understand the nature of hospitality work and evaluate whether it matches their interests and long-term career goals.

## Frequently Asked Questions About BHM in Nepal

### 1. What is the full form of BHM?

BHM stands for Bachelor of Hotel Management. It is an undergraduate programme focused on hotel operations, hospitality services and management.

### 2. How many years does BHM take in Nepal?

BHM generally takes four years to complete. Under the TU programme, it is divided into eight semesters.

### 3. How many credit hours are required for TU BHM?

The published Tribhuvan University BHM curriculum effective from the 2023 intake requires 126 credit hours.

### 4. What are the eligibility criteria for BHM in Nepal?

Students must complete 10+2 or an equivalent qualification and meet the academic requirements of their chosen university. TU has specific grade and CGPA requirements, which students should verify in the latest admission notice.

### 5. Is there an entrance exam for BHM in Nepal?

Students applying for the [TU Faculty of Managemen](https://www.tudoms.org/)t BHM programme must appear in the Central Management Admission Test (CMAT). Other universities may have different admission procedures.

### 6. What subjects are taught in BHM?

BHM includes subjects such as food production and patisserie, food and beverage service, housekeeping, front office operations, hotel engineering, accounting, marketing, financial management, entrepreneurship and hospitality management.

### 7. Does BHM include practical training?

Yes. Practical learning is an important component of BHM. The TU programme includes industrial exposure during the seventh and eighth semesters, along with an internship report and viva voce.

### 8. Can Science students study BHM?

Yes. Students from Science backgrounds may apply if they satisfy the admission requirements of the university offering the programme.

### 9. Can Management students study BHM?

Yes. Students from Management backgrounds can apply if they meet the applicable academic and entrance requirements.

### 10. What is the scope of BHM in Nepal?

BHM graduates can explore opportunities in hotels, resorts, restaurants, food and beverage operations, front office, housekeeping, event management, catering and other hospitality-related businesses.

### 11. Can BHM graduates work abroad?

Yes. BHM graduates may apply for hospitality-related jobs abroad, depending on the destination country's work regulations, employer requirements, professional experience and other eligibility conditions.

### 12. What can I study after BHM?

Graduates can consider postgraduate programmes such as MHM, MBA, tourism management and other hospitality-related master's degrees, depending on the entry requirements of the institution.

### 13. Is BHM better than BTTM?

Neither degree is universally better. BHM focuses more on hotel operations and hospitality management, while BTTM focuses on travel, tourism and destination-related services. The right choice depends on the student's career interests.

### 14. How much does BHM cost in Nepal?

BHM fees vary by university and college. Students should request the complete fee structure, including tuition, admission, examination, practical training and internship-related expenses, before enrolling.
