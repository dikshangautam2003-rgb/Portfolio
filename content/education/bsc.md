# BSc in Nepal: Course, Eligibility, Subjects, Fees & Career Scope

## What Is BSc in Nepal?

Bachelor of Science (BSc) is an undergraduate programme designed for students interested in scientific subjects, research, experimentation and analytical learning. The course provides a foundation in different areas of science through theoretical classes, laboratory practicals and research-based activities.

In Nepal, Tribhuvan University (TU) offers a four-year BSc General programme through its Institute of Science and Technology (IoST). Students can choose subjects from physical or biological science groups, depending on the available combinations at their chosen campus.

The programme is suitable for students who want to pursue higher studies or build careers in scientific research, education, laboratories, environmental studies and other science-related fields.

## Why Study BSc in Nepal?

BSc is a suitable option for students who want to develop a strong understanding of scientific concepts and explore different areas of science.

Some reasons to consider this programme include:

- **Scientific knowledge:** Develop an understanding of scientific principles, theories and practical applications.
- **Laboratory experience:** Gain practical exposure through experiments, laboratory sessions and scientific observation.
- **Research opportunities:** Develop research, data interpretation and analytical skills.
- **Subject flexibility:** Explore physical or biological science disciplines based on available subject combinations.
- **Higher education opportunities:** Build an academic foundation for postgraduate studies and specialised scientific programmes.

## BSc Course Duration and Structure

The general BSc programme under Tribhuvan University takes four years to complete and follows an annual academic system.

The curriculum is divided into four academic years, with a combination of core subjects, compulsory papers, electives, practical examinations and research activities.

Unlike semester-based programmes such as BSc CSIT, the general TU BSc programme follows an annual examination structure.

## BSc Subject Groups in Nepal

Students generally choose subjects from two broad science groups.

### 1. Physical Science Group

The physical science group focuses on mathematical, physical and earth-related scientific concepts.

Common subjects include:

- Physics
- Mathematics
- Statistics
- Geology

The exact combinations depend on the campus and available subject offerings.

### 2. Biological Science Group

The biological science group focuses on living organisms, biological systems and related scientific disciplines.

Common subjects include:

- Botany
- Zoology
- Chemistry
- Microbiology
- Geology

Not every college offers all subjects or combinations. Students should check the subject availability before applying.

## Practical Learning and Research

Practical learning is an important part of BSc education. Students apply scientific concepts through experiments, laboratory sessions, observations and research activities.

Depending on their chosen subjects and campus, practical learning may include:

- Laboratory experiments and demonstrations
- Scientific observation and data collection
- Field visits and fieldwork
- Research methodology and project preparation
- Statistical analysis and scientific reporting
- Practical examinations and presentations

The fourth year includes project or fieldwork options under the documented TU curriculum, helping students develop research and analytical capabilities.

## BSc Eligibility in Nepal

Students who have completed their higher secondary education in the Science stream can apply for BSc, subject to the admission requirements of their chosen university and campus.

For the general BSc programme under Tribhuvan University, the published eligibility criteria at TU campuses generally include:

- Completion of Grade 12 or an equivalent qualification in the Science stream.
- Minimum C grade in all subjects of Grades 11 and 12, under the applicable published criteria.
- Fulfilment of the subject-specific requirements for the selected science group.
- Successful participation in the required entrance examination.

Admission criteria and subject requirements may be updated. Students should check the latest IoST admission notice before applying.

## BSc Entrance Examination in Nepal

Tribhuvan University conducts an entrance examination through its Institute of Science and Technology for admission to its BSc programme.

Students should follow the current admission notice for:

- Application opening and closing dates
- Entrance examination schedule
- Examination syllabus and format
- Application fees
- Merit list and admission procedures

The examination requirements and selection process should be confirmed through the latest official notice.

## BSc Admission Process

The general admission process involves the following steps:

1. Check the latest TU IoST admission notice.
2. Confirm eligibility and subject requirements.
3. Select a campus offering the preferred BSc subject combination.
4. Complete the entrance application.
5. Appear in the entrance examination.
6. Check the merit list and complete admission formalities.

## BSc Fees in Nepal

The cost of studying BSc in Nepal depends on the university, campus, laboratory facilities and selected subjects.

Constituent and affiliated campuses may have different fee structures. Additional expenses may include admission charges, examination fees, laboratory charges, practical materials and other institutional costs.

Students should contact their preferred college for the latest fee breakdown and available scholarships.

There is no single fixed BSc fee applicable to all colleges in Nepal.

Students can explore several constituent and affiliated campuses offering BSc programmes under Tribhuvan University.

Some institutions include:

- **Tri-Chandra Multiple Campus, Kathmandu:** Offers general science education under TU.
- **Amrit Campus, Kathmandu:** A science-focused TU constituent campus.
- **St. Xavier's College, Kathmandu:** Offers science programmes including specialised BSc disciplines.
- **Central Campus of Technology, Dharan:** Offers BSc programmes in physical and biological science disciplines.

- **Siddhanath Science Campus, Mahendranagar:** Offers BSc General Biological Group.
- **New Horizon College, Butwal:** Offers BSc under TU affiliation.

Programme availability and subject combinations vary. Students should confirm whether the college offers general BSc or a specialised BSc programme before applying.

## Frequently Asked Questions About BSc in Nepal

### 1. What is the full form of BSc?

BSc stands for Bachelor of Science.

### 2. How many years does BSc take in Nepal?

The general BSc programme under Tribhuvan University takes four years and follows an annual academic structure.

### 3. What are the main subjects in BSc?

Students can study subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Statistics, Botany, Zoology, Geology and Microbiology, depending on their selected group and campus.

### 4. What is the eligibility for BSc in Nepal?

Students generally need to complete Grade 12 in the Science stream or an equivalent qualification and meet the university's academic and entrance requirements.

### 5. Is an entrance examination required for BSc?

Yes. Tribhuvan University's general BSc admission process requires students to appear in the entrance examination conducted through IoST.

### 6. What is the difference between physical and biological science groups?

The physical group focuses on subjects such as Physics, Mathematics, Statistics and Geology, while the biological group focuses on subjects such as Botany, Zoology, Chemistry and related life sciences.

### 7. What are the career opportunities after BSc?

Graduates can explore research, education, laboratory, environmental and other science-related career opportunities, depending on their specialisation and qualifications.

### 8. Can I pursue an MSc after BSc?

Yes. BSc graduates can apply for relevant MSc programmes, subject to the admission and subject-specific requirements of the university.

### 9. Is BSc different from BSc CSIT?

Yes. General BSc focuses on scientific disciplines, whereas BSc CSIT focuses on computer science and information technology.
