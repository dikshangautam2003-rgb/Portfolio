# BIM in Nepal: Course, Fees, Syllabus and Career Scope

Choosing an undergraduate course after +2 can be challenging, especially when you are interested in both technology and business. If you want to understand how software, information systems, databases and business operations work together, the Bachelor of Information Management (BIM) is one programme worth considering.

**BIM in Nepal is a four-year undergraduate programme that combines Information Technology (IT), computing, business management and analytical subjects.** Under Tribhuvan University's Faculty of Management, the programme follows an eight-semester structure and the revised curriculum requires students to complete 126 credit hours, including IT and computing courses, management subjects, electives, a project and an internship.

Unlike a programme focused entirely on computer science or general business administration, BIM connects technical knowledge with the practical needs of organisations. Students learn programming, web technologies, databases, networking, information security and business management to understand how technology can solve organisational problems.

This guide covers the BIM course in Nepal, including its duration, eligibility, CMAT entrance examination, semester-wise syllabus, fees, colleges, career opportunities, salary considerations and future scope.

## What Is BIM?

BIM stands for **Bachelor of Information Management**. It is an undergraduate degree designed to combine information technology with business and management education.

The programme focuses on developing graduates who can understand computing systems and apply technological knowledge to real-world organisational and business challenges.

Students study subjects such as programming, database management, web technology, software development, networking, information security, business statistics, accounting, marketing and strategic management.

For example, a company may need a system to manage customer information, sales records, inventory and financial reports. A BIM graduate can understand both the technical requirements of such systems and the business processes they are intended to support.

The programme is particularly relevant for students interested in working between technical teams and business departments.

## Why Study BIM in Nepal?

BIM can be a suitable choice for students who want a combination of computing and management rather than specialising in only one area.

Some reasons students consider this programme include:

### 1. Combination of IT and Management

BIM introduces students to programming, databases, software development and information systems alongside management, accounting, marketing and business strategy.

This combination helps students understand how technology supports organisational operations and decision-making.

### 2. Practical and Technical Learning

The curriculum includes programming, web development, database management, networking, software design and development, information security and project work.

These subjects provide a foundation for developing and managing technology-based solutions.

### 3. Understanding Business Applications of Technology

Technology is used across banking, finance, retail, education, healthcare, telecommunications and other industries.

BIM helps students understand how information systems can support business processes, manage data and improve organisational efficiency.

### 4. Different Career Directions

Depending on their technical skills, practical experience and professional interests, graduates can explore opportunities in software development, web development, database management, IT support, business analysis and information systems.

### 5. Foundation for Further Education

BIM graduates can consider postgraduate education in information technology, information systems, management, business administration and related fields, subject to the eligibility requirements of the institution and programme.

## BIM Course Duration and Academic Structure

The BIM programme offered by Tribhuvan University is completed over four academic years, divided into eight semesters.

Each semester covers a combination of technical, analytical and management subjects. As students progress, the programme moves from foundational computing and business concepts towards more specialised areas such as software development, information security, artificial intelligence, business intelligence and IT entrepreneurship.

The revised TU curriculum requires students to complete 126 credit hours.

## Practical Learning, Project Work and Internship in BIM

BIM is not limited to theoretical knowledge. Its curriculum includes technical exercises, project work and an organisational internship.

### Programming and Laboratory Work

Students develop programming and technical problem-solving skills through subjects such as C Programming, Java, Python, web technologies, databases and networking.

### Project Work

The curriculum includes a dedicated project component worth three credit hours. It gives students an opportunity to apply their knowledge to a selected technical or information-system problem.

### Internship

The final semester includes an internship worth three credit hours. Under the TU curriculum, students are attached to an organisation for eight weeks.

Students prepare an individual project report based on their work experience. Internship evaluation includes the organisation's confidential report, the project report and its presentation.

This experience helps students understand professional working environments and apply academic knowledge to practical tasks.

## Frequently Asked Questions About BIM in Nepal

### 1. What is the full form of BIM?

BIM stands for Bachelor of Information Management. It is an undergraduate programme that combines information technology, computing and business management.

### 2. How many years does BIM take in Nepal?

The TU BIM programme takes four years to complete and is divided into eight semesters.

### 3. How many credit hours are required for BIM?

The revised Tribhuvan University BIM curriculum requires students to complete 126 credit hours, including management, analytical, IT, elective, project and internship components.

### 4. Is BIM an IT or management course?

BIM combines both fields. It includes programming, databases, networking and information systems alongside business management, accounting, marketing and strategic management.

### 5. What is the eligibility for BIM in Nepal?

Under the TU curriculum, applicants must complete twelve years of schooling or an equivalent qualification recognised by TU, with the prescribed minimum grades or second division. They must also complete the required admission procedures.

### 6. Is there an entrance exam for BIM?

Yes. Admission to the TU BIM programme requires eligible applicants to appear in the Central Management Admission Test (CMAT) and meet the applicable selection criteria.

### 7. Is mathematics compulsory for BIM?

The BIM curriculum includes Basic Mathematics, Discrete Structure and Business Statistics. Students should be comfortable developing mathematical and analytical skills.

### 8. Can management students study BIM?

Students from management backgrounds can apply if they meet the university's admission eligibility requirements. The programme includes both management and technical subjects, so they should be prepared to study programming and computing.

### 9. What are the major subjects in BIM?

Major subjects include C Programming, Java Programming, Python Programming, Web Technology, Database Management System, Software Design and Development, Artificial Intelligence, Information Security, Business Information Systems and Business Intelligence.

### 10. What jobs can I get after BIM?

Depending on their skills and experience, graduates can pursue roles such as software developer, web developer, database administrator, system analyst, business analyst, IT support officer and information systems officer.

### 11. Can I study abroad after BIM?

Yes. BIM graduates can apply for relevant postgraduate programmes abroad, subject to the academic requirements of the university, programme prerequisites and other admission conditions.

### 12. Is BIM better than BCA?

Neither course is universally better. BIM combines IT and management, while [BCA](https://dikshangautam.com.np/education/academic-courses/bca/) focuses more directly on computer applications and software development. Students should compare the curricula and their career interests before deciding.
