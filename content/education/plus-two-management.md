# +2 Management in Nepal: Subjects, Eligibility, Colleges and Career Scope

Choosing the right subjects after SEE is an important decision for students planning their higher education. For those interested in business, finance, accounting, entrepreneurship and organisational management, +2 Management is a popular academic pathway in Nepal.

The programme introduces students to business concepts, financial systems, economic activities and management principles. It also provides a foundation for pursuing bachelor's degrees in business administration, business studies, management, hospitality and related fields.

This guide explains +2 Management in Nepal, including its subjects, eligibility requirements, course structure, admission process, fees, higher study options and career opportunities.

## What Is +2 Management in Nepal?

+2 Management is a commonly used name for the Grade 11 and Grade 12 academic pathway focused on business, accounting, economics and management-related subjects. It is a two-year programme conducted under the National Examinations Board (NEB).

Students are introduced to how businesses operate, how financial transactions are recorded, how markets function and how organisations manage their resources.

The programme is suitable for students who want to explore business, banking, finance, entrepreneurship, hospitality, marketing and administration.

## Why Choose +2 Management?

+2 Management provides an introduction to how businesses, financial institutions and organisations work. It helps students understand commercial activities while developing skills useful for further education and professional life.

Some reasons students choose this pathway include:

- **Foundation in business:** Students learn the basic principles of business operations, management and organisational activities.
- **Accounting and financial knowledge:** The programme introduces financial transactions, accounting concepts and economic decision-making.
- **Career flexibility:** Students can explore different academic areas, including business administration, finance, banking, hospitality and information management.
- **Entrepreneurial understanding:** Students gain an introductory understanding of business planning, marketing and entrepreneurship.
- **Preparation for higher education:** The programme creates a foundation for bachelor's-level courses in management and related disciplines.

## Subjects in +2 Management in Nepal

The subjects offered under +2 Management depend on the applicable NEB curriculum, subject selection requirements and the combinations available at individual schools or colleges.

Common subject areas include Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies, Business Mathematics, Marketing, Finance and Computer Science.

### Common Subjects in +2 Management

**1. Accountancy**

Accountancy introduces students to the process of recording, classifying and summarising financial transactions. Students learn basic accounting principles and how financial information is prepared and interpreted.

Topics may include:

- Accounting principles and concepts
- Journal and ledger entries
- Trial balance
- Financial statements
- Accounting adjustments

**2. Economics**

Economics helps students understand how individuals, businesses and governments make decisions about limited resources.

Students explore concepts such as demand, supply, markets, production, consumption and economic development.

**3. Business Studies**

Business Studies introduces students to the structure, functions and operations of businesses. It explains how organisations are established, managed and developed.

Common areas include:

- Nature and objectives of business
- Forms of business organisation
- Business management
- Marketing and business operations
- Entrepreneurship

**4. Business Mathematics**

Business Mathematics applies mathematical concepts to business and financial situations. It helps students develop numerical and analytical skills.

Topics may include percentages, interest, profit and loss, statistics and mathematical applications in business decisions.

**5. Marketing**

Marketing introduces students to how businesses identify customer needs, promote products and services, and develop relationships with customers.

Students learn about market research, product development, pricing, promotion and distribution.

**6. Finance**

Finance introduces the principles of managing money, investments and financial resources. It helps students understand how individuals and organisations make financial decisions.

**7. Computer Science**

Some schools and colleges offer Computer Science as an elective option. It introduces students to computing concepts, digital systems and basic programming or practical computer applications, depending on the curriculum.

## Eligibility Criteria for +2 Management in Nepal

Students who have completed the Secondary Education Examination (SEE) or an equivalent qualification can apply for Grade 11 programmes.

Admission requirements may differ between institutions. Some colleges consider SEE grades, subject-specific requirements, entrance examinations and interviews during the admission process.

Before applying, students should:

- Complete SEE or an equivalent qualification.
- Check the institution's minimum academic requirements.
- Review any subject-specific prerequisites.
- Confirm the available subject combinations.
- Complete the admission application and submit the required documents.

Students applying with qualifications from other education boards may also need an equivalence certificate, depending on the institution and applicable rules.

## Admission Process for +2 Management

The admission process generally begins after SEE results are published. Students can apply to schools and colleges offering Grade 11 Management programmes.

A typical admission process includes:

1. **Research colleges:** Compare institutions based on academic quality, location, facilities and available subjects.
2. **Check eligibility:** Review the institution's academic and admission requirements.
3. **Complete the application:** Obtain and submit the admission form.
4. **Attend an entrance examination:** Some institutions conduct entrance tests or interviews.
5. **Submit documents:** Provide SEE results, certificates, photographs and other required documents.
6. **Confirm admission:** Complete the required admission formalities and fee payment.

The cost of studying +2 Management varies depending on the school or college, its location, facilities, academic services and scholarship provisions.

Before selecting a college, students should ask for a complete fee breakdown, including:

## What Can You Study After +2 Management?

Completing +2 Management opens pathways to several bachelor's-level programmes in business, management, finance, hospitality and related fields.

The appropriate degree depends on the student's interests, subject background and the admission requirements of the university or institution.

### Popular Bachelor's Courses After +2 Management

**1. Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)**

BBA focuses on business administration, organisational management, marketing, finance and leadership. It is suitable for students interested in managing businesses and working in corporate environments.

**2. Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS)**

BBS provides an academic foundation in business, accounting, finance, economics and management. It is a common choice for students interested in business and financial studies.

**3. Bachelor of Business Management (BBM)**

BBM focuses on business management, leadership, organisational operations and decision-making. It is suitable for students interested in management-related careers.

**4. Bachelor of Information Management (BIM)**

BIM combines management education with information technology. It is an option for students interested in business systems, information management and technology-related organisational roles, subject to admission requirements.

**5. Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM)**

BHM focuses on hospitality operations, hotel administration, food and beverage services and tourism-related management.

**6. Bachelor of Travel and Tourism Management**

This programme introduces students to tourism operations, travel services, destination management and hospitality-related business activities.

**7. Bachelor of Economics**

Students interested in economic analysis, financial systems, public policy and research may consider economics-related bachelor's programmes, depending on university eligibility requirements.

## Is +2 Management Difficult?

The difficulty of +2 Management depends on a student's interests, learning habits and understanding of the subjects.

Students who enjoy business concepts, financial calculations, economics and organisational activities may find the programme engaging.

Accountancy and Business Mathematics require regular practice, while Economics and Business Studies involve understanding concepts and applying them to different situations.

Students can manage the coursework by:

- Practising accounting problems regularly.
- Understanding concepts instead of memorising everything.
- Following current business and economic developments.
- Revising lessons consistently.
- Asking teachers for help when concepts are unclear.

A genuine interest in business and a consistent study routine can make the programme more manageable.

## Who Should Choose +2 Management?

+2 Management may be a suitable option for students who:

- Are interested in business, accounting and finance.
- Want to understand how organisations operate.
- Have an interest in entrepreneurship and business development.
- Enjoy working with numbers and analysing information.
- Want to pursue bachelor's degrees in business or management.
- Are interested in banking, marketing, hospitality or administration.

Students should consider their interests and future academic goals before choosing their subjects. Selecting a programme only because friends or family members recommend it may not be the best approach.

## Frequently Asked Questions About +2 Management in Nepal

### 1. How many years does +2 Management take in Nepal?

+2 Management covers Grade 11 and Grade 12 and generally takes two academic years to complete.

### 2. What are the main subjects in +2 Management?

Common subject areas include Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies, Business Mathematics, Marketing, Finance and Computer Science. The actual combination depends on the curriculum and the institution.

### 3. Is Mathematics compulsory in +2 Management?

Mathematics is not necessarily compulsory for every Management combination. Under the general Grade 11–12 structure, students may have Social Studies and Life Skills Education or Mathematics as a compulsory subject option. Business Mathematics may also be available as an elective, depending on the subject combination.

### 4. Can I study BBA after +2 Management?

Yes. Students who complete Grade 12 can apply for [BBA](https://dikshangautam.com.np/education/academic-courses/bba-in-nepal/) programmes if they meet the university's academic and entrance requirements.

### 5. Can I study BCA after +2 Management?

Students from Management backgrounds may be eligible for some [BCA](https://dikshangautam.com.np/education/academic-courses/bca/) programmes, depending on the university's current admission requirements and subject prerequisites. Students should verify the requirements before applying.

### 6. Can I study BIM after +2 Management?

Yes, students may apply for [BIM](https://dikshangautam.com.np/education/academic-courses/bim-in-nepal/) programmes if they meet the relevant university's eligibility and admission requirements.

### 7. What are the career opportunities after +2 Management?
