# BBS in Nepal: Complete Guide to Course, Fees, Colleges, Eligibility and Career Scope

Choosing the right bachelor's degree after +2 is an important decision for students planning their academic and professional future. If you are interested in business, accounting, finance, marketing, economics and management, a Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) can be a suitable undergraduate option.

BBS in Nepal is a four-year undergraduate management program offered by Tribhuvan University's Faculty of Management through its constituent and affiliated campuses. Unlike semester-based management programs such as BBA and BBM, the TU BBS program follows an annual examination system, with students completing one academic year at a time.

The course provides a foundation in business and management while allowing students to develop knowledge in areas such as accounting, finance, marketing and management through concentration courses in the final year.

This guide explains everything you need to know about studying BBS in Nepal, including course duration, eligibility, admission process, syllabus, subjects, fees, colleges, specialisation options, career opportunities and higher studies after graduation.

## BBS Course Duration and Structure in Nepal

The BBS program under Tribhuvan University's Faculty of Management takes four academic years to complete.

It follows an annual examination system rather than the semester system used by programs such as BBA and BBM.

The curriculum includes compulsory and core courses, concentration subjects and a final project.

### How Does the BBS Annual System Work?

Unlike semester-based programs, BBS is organised around four academic years.

Students study the prescribed subjects for each year and appear in the annual examinations conducted by Tribhuvan University.

The published curriculum specifies a minimum of 150 teaching days in an academic year and a minimum of 70% attendance in classes actually held for examination eligibility.

Students should check the latest examination notices and campus requirements for their academic batch.

## BBS Eligibility Criteria in Nepal

Students who have completed +2 or an equivalent qualification can apply for the BBS program if they meet the admission requirements set by Tribhuvan University and the concerned campus.

The official BBS curriculum identifies the following academic requirements:

- Completion of 10+2 in Business or Commerce, or an equivalent qualification recognised by Tribhuvan University.
- Alternatively, students may qualify through the prescribed Mathematics or Economics subject requirements at the +2 level.
- English must have been studied as a full paper at the +2 or equivalent level.
- Applicants must meet the minimum marks and any additional admission criteria prescribed by the Faculty of Management or the concerned campus.

A published TU admission procedure for annual BBS programs specifies a minimum of 45% or a D grade equivalent to 1.8 CGPA. However, students should confirm the latest admission notice and the subject requirements of their selected campus before applying.

### Can Management Students Study BBS?

Yes. Students who have completed +2 Management or an equivalent Business or Commerce qualification can apply for BBS if they meet the required academic criteria.

### Can Science Students Study BBS?

Students from Science backgrounds may be eligible if they meet the subject requirements specified in the BBS curriculum, including the relevant Mathematics or Economics requirement and English requirement.

They should confirm their eligibility with the selected campus before applying.

### Can Humanities Students Study BBS?

Eligibility depends on the subjects studied at the +2 level and the current admission criteria. Students should check whether their academic background satisfies the Mathematics or Economics and English requirements prescribed for the program.

## BBS Admission Process in Nepal

The admission process for BBS is generally managed by the individual constituent or affiliated campus under Tribhuvan University.

Unlike TU's BBA and BBM programs, which use the Central Management Admission Test (CMAT), BBS follows its own admission arrangements. Students should not assume that the same entrance examination procedure applies to every management program.

The general admission process involves the following steps.

### Step 1: Check Your Eligibility

Review the academic requirements and confirm that your +2 qualification and subject combination meet the requirements of the selected campus.

### Step 2: Select a BBS College

Research colleges offering BBS under Tribhuvan University. Compare location, academic facilities, teaching arrangements, fees and student support.

### Step 3: Complete the Application

Obtain the admission form from the selected campus or its official website and submit it with the required documents.

### Step 4: Follow the College Selection Process

Some campuses may apply additional admission criteria or screening procedures. Follow the latest admission notice published by the institution.

### Step 5: Complete Admission and Registration

After selection, complete the required admission formalities, submit the necessary documents and pay the applicable charges.

### Documents Commonly Required for BBS Admission

Students may be asked to submit:

- SEE or Grade 10 marksheet and certificate
- Grade 11 and Grade 12 transcripts
- Character certificate
- Migration certificate, where applicable
- Citizenship certificate or accepted identification document
- Passport-size photographs
- Other documents specified by the selected campus

Always confirm the final document list with the college.
