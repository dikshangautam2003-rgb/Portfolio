# BE in Nepal: Courses, Eligibility, Entrance Examination, Colleges and Career Opportunities

Engineering is one of the most sought-after professional education pathways for students interested in technology, infrastructure, innovation and problem-solving. From designing roads and buildings to developing software, communication systems, machines and electrical networks, engineers contribute to the development of modern society.

**BE in Nepal (Bachelor of Engineering)** is an undergraduate degree designed to develop the technical knowledge, practical abilities and analytical skills required for engineering careers. Students can choose from several engineering disciplines, including Civil Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Software Engineering and other specialised fields.

The programme is offered by different universities and engineering institutions across Nepal. However, the admission process, entrance examination, curriculum, tuition fees and available engineering branches depend on the university and college.

This guide covers everything students need to know about studying BE in Nepal, including course duration, engineering specialisations, eligibility, entrance examinations, admission procedures, colleges, fees, scholarships, career opportunities and higher education options.

## What Is BE in Nepal?

BE stands for Bachelor of Engineering. It is an undergraduate academic and professional degree that focuses on applying scientific principles, mathematics and technical knowledge to solve real-world engineering problems.

In Nepal, BE programmes are offered through universities such as Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu University, Pokhara University and Purbanchal University, along with other recognised institutions.

The programme combines theoretical learning with practical laboratory work, technical projects, field studies, design exercises and, depending on the programme, internships or industry-related activities.

Students select an engineering discipline based on their interests and academic strengths. For example:

- Civil Engineering focuses on infrastructure, construction and structural development.
- Computer Engineering combines computing, electronics and hardware-software systems.
- Electrical Engineering focuses on electrical power generation, transmission and distribution.
- Electronics and Communication Engineering deals with electronic devices, communication networks and signal systems.
- Mechanical Engineering focuses on machines, manufacturing, energy systems and mechanical design.
- Software Engineering focuses on the systematic design, development, testing and maintenance of software systems.

The degree prepares students for technical employment, professional engineering practice, research and postgraduate education.

## Why Study BE in Nepal?

Engineering offers students an opportunity to develop technical expertise while working on practical challenges in different industries.

Some reasons students choose a Bachelor of Engineering include:

### 1. Diverse Engineering Specialisations

Students can select an engineering branch according to their interests. Options range from infrastructure and construction to computing, electronics, energy, manufacturing and software development.

### 2. Practical and Technical Learning

Engineering education involves more than classroom lectures. Students participate in laboratory experiments, technical assignments, design projects, field visits and practical exercises that help connect theoretical concepts with real applications.

### 3. Opportunities in Different Industries

Engineering graduates can explore employment in construction, hydropower, information technology, telecommunications, manufacturing, transportation, energy, consulting and other technical sectors.

### 4. Development of Problem-Solving Skills

Engineering students learn to analyse problems, evaluate possible solutions, work with technical information and make decisions based on scientific and mathematical principles.

### 5. Opportunities for Higher Education

After completing BE, graduates can pursue postgraduate studies in specialised engineering fields, technology, research, management and related disciplines in Nepal or abroad.

### 6. Contribution to National Development

Nepal continues to require technical professionals for infrastructure development, energy projects, urban planning, digital services, communication systems and industrial growth.

## Different Types of BE Courses in Nepal

Students can choose from several engineering disciplines. Each branch has a different academic focus, technical curriculum and professional direction.

The availability of a particular programme depends on the university and affiliated college.

Civil Engineering focuses on planning, designing, constructing and maintaining infrastructure and the built environment.

Students learn about structures, construction materials, soil mechanics, transportation systems, water resources, surveying and environmental considerations.

## Eligibility Criteria for BE in Nepal

Students generally need to complete +2 Science or an equivalent qualification to apply for a Bachelor of Engineering programme.

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics are important academic requirements for many conventional engineering programmes.

However, the exact eligibility criteria differ between universities.

### General Academic Requirements

Students may need to meet the following requirements:

- Complete Grade 12 in Science or an equivalent recognised qualification.
- Study the required combination of Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
- Meet the minimum GPA, percentage or subject-grade requirements set by the university.
- Pass the relevant university entrance examination.
- Meet any additional requirements for the selected engineering programme.

### Eligibility for TU/IOE Engineering Admission

The Institute of Engineering (IOE), Tribhuvan University, publishes its own entrance eligibility requirements.

The IOE admission criteria have included +2 Science or an equivalent qualification with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. The published admission information also specifies minimum academic performance requirements, including the required subject grades or aggregate marks.

Applicants should check the latest IOE admission notice for the applicable academic session because eligibility rules can be updated. [Official IOE/TU admission information](https://portal.tu.edu.np/downloads/EngineeringCollege2081_2024_09_02_10_27_58.pdf).

### Eligibility for Kathmandu University

Kathmandu University uses its own admission process and the Kathmandu University Common Admission Test (KUCAT-CBT) for relevant engineering programmes.

For its 2026/2027 undergraduate admission call, KU specified a minimum aggregate GPA of 2.0 or 50% marks in Grade 12 or equivalent, alongside programme-specific subject requirements. Several BE programmes require Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in both Grades 11 and 12. Applicants should consult the current programme-specific notice before applying. [Kathmandu University admission notice](https://apply.ku.edu.np/kucat26admissionCall.php).

### Can Biology Students Study Engineering?

Students from a Biology background should not assume that they automatically qualify for every engineering programme.

Some universities and programmes require Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Kathmandu University's 2026/2027 admission information, for example, states that Biology-stream students applying for PCM test eligibility must have passed Additional Mathematics.

Eligibility depends on the university, the programme and the applicant's actual subject combination. Students should verify their eligibility before preparing for an entrance examination.

## BE Entrance Examination in Nepal

Engineering colleges and universities generally use entrance examinations or merit-based admission procedures to select undergraduate engineering students.

The entrance examination assesses academic knowledge, analytical ability and readiness for engineering education.

The examination process depends on the university.

### 1. IOE Entrance Examination

The Institute of Engineering under Tribhuvan University conducts the entrance examination for its BE and B.Arch programmes.

The examination is an important part of the admission process for students seeking admission to IOE constituent and affiliated colleges.

Preparation generally involves:

- Physics
- Chemistry
- Mathematics
- English
- Logical and analytical problem-solving

The exact examination format, question distribution, marking scheme, eligibility criteria, application deadlines and admission rules should be checked in the latest IOE entrance notice.

### 2. Kathmandu University Common Admission Test (KUCAT-CBT)

Kathmandu University conducts the Kathmandu University Common Admission Test in computer-based format for relevant undergraduate programmes.

The test group depends on the programme. Engineering applicants commonly need the PCM group, covering Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

KU's 2026/2027 admission notice lists BE programmes such as Civil, Computer, Electrical and Electronics, Mechanical, Chemical, Mining and Environmental Engineering, with programme-specific subject requirements. [KU undergraduate admission portal](https://apply.ku.edu.np/kucat26admissionCall.php).

### 3. Pokhara University Entrance Examination

Pokhara University has its own admission procedure for engineering programmes offered through its School of Engineering and affiliated institutions.

The university publishes admission notices, entrance examination procedures, intake information and merit lists.

Students should follow the latest admission guidelines for the relevant academic year. [Pokhara University School of Engineering](https://soe.pu.edu.np/).

### 4. Entrance Examinations of Other Universities

Other universities, including Purbanchal University and institutions operating under other recognised universities, may have their own admission and selection procedures.

Students should not assume that passing one university's entrance examination automatically guarantees admission to another university's engineering programme.

## Admission Process for BE in Nepal

Although the admission procedure varies between universities, the general process involves the following steps.

**Step 1: Complete +2 Science or an equivalent qualification**

Students should complete Grade 12 with the academic subjects required by their intended engineering programme.

**Step 2: Select an engineering branch**

Explore different disciplines and identify the branch that matches your interests, academic strengths and future career plans.

**Step 3: Choose a university**

Compare universities based on their engineering programmes, curriculum, academic facilities, recognition, entrance examination and available colleges.

**Step 4: Check entrance eligibility and notices**

Read the official admission notice carefully. Check the subject requirements, minimum grades, application deadline, examination format and required documents.

**Step 5: Submit the entrance application**

Complete the online application form and submit the required academic and personal information.

**Step 6: Prepare for and appear in the entrance examination**

Study the relevant subjects and follow the official examination syllabus and guidelines.

**Step 7: Check the merit list and admission options**

After the examination, check the published merit list, admission ranking and available seats.

**Step 8: Complete college admission**

Select an eligible college and programme according to the admission rules. Complete document verification, registration and fee payment.

## Scholarships for BE Students in Nepal

Students may be eligible for scholarships and financial assistance depending on their academic performance, entrance ranking, admission category and the policies of the university or college.

Common scholarship opportunities include:

- Merit-based scholarships for students with strong entrance examination performance.
- Reserved or inclusion quotas according to applicable admission rules.
- Need-based financial assistance offered by institutions.
- Tuition fee discounts provided by colleges.
- Scholarships announced by universities or government-related bodies.

Scholarship availability and conditions vary. Some awards cover tuition fees, while others may provide partial discounts or financial assistance.

Students should check the latest university admission notice and scholarship guidelines instead of relying on previous years' scholarship arrangements.

## BE vs BTech: What Is the Difference?

BE and BTech are both undergraduate engineering qualifications, but their naming conventions, academic structures and programme requirements depend on the university and country.

Traditionally, BE programmes are often associated with engineering principles and design, while BTech programmes are sometimes described as more technology- or application-oriented.

However, the distinction is not universal. The actual curriculum, recognition, accreditation, professional eligibility and academic outcomes matter more than the degree title alone.

Students should compare the specific university and programme rather than assuming one qualification is automatically better than the other.

## Frequently Asked Questions About BE in Nepal

### 1. What is BE in Nepal?

BE stands for Bachelor of Engineering. It is an undergraduate degree that develops technical knowledge and practical skills in engineering disciplines such as Civil, Computer, Electrical, Mechanical and Software Engineering.

### 2. How many years is BE in Nepal?

Most conventional BE programmes in Nepal take four years and are divided into eight semesters. The duration may differ for specialised programmes.

### 3. What is the eligibility for BE after +2?

Students generally need to complete +2 Science or an equivalent qualification with the required Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics subjects. Minimum grades, aggregate marks and entrance requirements depend on the university.

### 4. Can Biology students study BE in Nepal?

It depends on the university and engineering programme. Many programmes require Mathematics alongside Physics and Chemistry. Some universities may allow Biology-stream students who have completed Additional Mathematics and meet the other eligibility requirements.

### 5. Is an entrance examination required for BE in Nepal?

Most universities use an entrance examination or a defined admission selection process. Examples include the IOE entrance examination under Tribhuvan University and KUCAT-CBT under Kathmandu University.

### 6. What is the IOE entrance examination?

The IOE entrance examination is conducted by the Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, for admission to its undergraduate engineering and architecture programmes. Students should check the latest entrance notice for the syllabus, examination format and eligibility requirements.

### 7. Which BE branch is best in Nepal?

There is no single best engineering branch for every student. Civil, Computer, Electrical, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical, Software and other disciplines offer different academic and career pathways. The right choice depends on individual interests, abilities and professional goals.

### 8. How much does it cost to study BE in Nepal?

The total cost varies according to the university, college, engineering branch and admission category. Constituent campuses, affiliated colleges and private institutions may have different fee structures. Students should request the latest complete fee breakdown before admission.

### 9. Are scholarships available for BE in Nepal?

Yes. Universities and colleges may offer merit-based scholarships, reserved-seat opportunities, financial assistance and tuition discounts. Eligibility and scholarship coverage vary by institution and admission year.

### 10. Which university is best for BE in Nepal?

Students can compare Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu University, Pokhara University, Purbanchal University and other recognised universities. The best option depends on the chosen engineering branch, curriculum, facilities, fees, faculty, recognition and location.

### 11. Can I study Computer Engineering after +2 Management?

Conventional Computer Engineering programmes generally require Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics or an accepted equivalent qualification. A +2 Management background usually does not meet these standard entry requirements. Students should check the specific university's eligibility rules.

### 12. What is the difference between Computer Engineering and Software Engineering?

Computer Engineering covers both hardware and software, including computer architecture, electronics, embedded systems and programming. Software Engineering focuses more specifically on software design, development, testing, deployment and maintenance.

### 13. What can I study after completing BE?

Graduates can pursue ME, MSc in Engineering, specialised technology programmes, research degrees and engineering management courses. The available options depend on the graduate's engineering discipline and university admission criteria.

### 14. Can BE graduates work abroad?

Yes. BE graduates can explore employment and postgraduate education opportunities in other countries. Requirements depend on the destination country's professional regulations, qualification recognition, experience, language proficiency and employer expectations.

### 15. Is Nepal Engineering Council registration necessary?

NEC registration is an important professional requirement for engineers in Nepal. Graduates should check the applicable registration rules and eligibility conditions for their qualification and intended professional practice.

### 16. Can students study BE after a Diploma in Engineering?

Some universities offer admission routes or related programmes for eligible diploma holders. However, the accepted diploma, entry level, credit transfer and eligibility requirements vary. Students should consult the relevant university's current admission notice.

### 17. Is BE and B.Arch the same?

No. BE programmes focus on engineering disciplines, while B.Arch (Bachelor of Architecture) focuses on architectural design, planning, built environments and related technical subjects. Their course duration and professional requirements also differ.

### 18. Does every engineering college offer all BE branches?

No. Colleges offer different engineering disciplines depending on their university affiliation, academic approval, faculty, laboratories and available resources. Students should confirm the specific programme and current intake before applying.
