**+2 Humanities in Nepal** is a two-year higher secondary study pathway covering Grade 11 and Grade 12. It introduces students to different aspects of human society and social sciences while developing communication, analytical thinking, research and problem-solving skills.

Students interested in journalism, law, social work, psychology, education, public administration, development studies and related fields may consider Humanities as a suitable academic direction.

## What Is +2 Humanities in Nepal?

+2 Humanities is a higher secondary education pathway that focuses on understanding human society, culture, behaviour, communication and social institutions.

The programme covers two academic years: Grade 11 and Grade 12. Students study compulsory subjects alongside selected electives that allow them to explore their academic interests.

Unlike Science, which focuses heavily on scientific concepts, or Management, which emphasises business and financial studies, Humanities provides a broader understanding of people, communities, social relationships and human development.

The programme is particularly relevant for students who enjoy reading, writing, research, communication, critical thinking and analysing social issues.

In Nepal, the National Examinations Board (NEB) conducts the Grade 11 and Grade 12 examinations. However, it is important to understand that the current national curriculum follows compulsory and elective subject selection rather than rigid, officially fixed academic streams. Colleges may still use labels such as Science, Management and Humanities to describe their programmes and subject combinations.

## Why Choose +2 Humanities?

Humanities provides an opportunity to understand how people interact, how societies function and how social, cultural and political systems influence everyday life.

Some important reasons to consider this academic pathway include:

**1. Understanding society and human behaviour**

Humanities introduces students to social relationships, cultures, communities, institutions and human behaviour. Students learn how different social factors influence individuals and society.

**2. Developing communication skills**

Subjects involving languages, literature and mass communication help students improve their writing, speaking, presentation and interpersonal communication abilities.

**3. Improving critical thinking**

Students explore different perspectives, analyse social issues and develop the ability to evaluate information instead of simply memorising facts.

**4. Exploring diverse academic opportunities**

Humanities can lead to higher studies in fields such as Social Work, Journalism, Law, Education, Sociology, Psychology and Development Studies, depending on university admission requirements.

**5. Understanding social and global issues**

Students learn about topics such as population, migration, inequality, governance, cultural diversity, human rights and community development.

## Eligibility Criteria for +2 Humanities in Nepal

- Submission of the SEE marksheet or transcript.
- Submission of the character certificate.
- Completion of the college admission application.
- Meeting any additional requirements specified by the institution.

Individual colleges may have their own admission procedures, entrance assessments, scholarship criteria or academic requirements.

Students should check the latest admission notice before applying.
