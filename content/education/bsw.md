# BSW in Nepal: Course, Fees, Syllabus & Career Scope

## What Is BSW in Nepal?

Bachelor of Social Work (BSW) is an undergraduate programme designed for students interested in understanding social issues and contributing to positive changes in society. It focuses on working with individuals, families, groups and communities through professional social work methods.

The BSW course in Nepal combines theoretical learning with practical exposure. Students study subjects related to sociology, psychology, social policies, community development, human rights, research and social work practices.

The programme is suitable for students who want to build careers in social development, NGOs, INGOs, community organisations, research and social welfare services.

## Why Study BSW in Nepal?

BSW is a suitable choice for students who want to understand social challenges and work towards improving people's lives.

Some reasons to consider this programme include:

- **Understanding society:** Learn about social structures, inequality, poverty, culture and community challenges.
- **Practical experience:** Apply classroom knowledge through fieldwork and community engagement.
- **Professional skills:** Develop communication, research, problem-solving and interpersonal skills.
- **Career diversity:** Explore opportunities in development organisations, social welfare institutions and research.
- **Social contribution:** Work on issues involving education, health, human rights, inclusion and community development.

## BSW Course Duration and Structure

The BSW programme generally takes four years to complete. Under Tribhuvan University's documented semester-based BSW curriculum, the course is divided into eight semesters.

The curriculum combines core social work subjects with related disciplines, practical fieldwork, research and specialised areas of study.

## Practical Learning and Fieldwork

Fieldwork is an important part of social work education. It helps students understand real community situations and apply the theories and methods learned in class.

Depending on the university, practical learning may include:

- Community visits and assessments
- Social casework and group work
- Agency placements
- Community organisation activities
- Research and report writing
- Social awareness and development projects

This practical exposure helps students develop professional communication, observation, documentation and problem-solving skills.

## BSW Eligibility in Nepal

Students who have completed their higher secondary education can apply for BSW, subject to the admission requirements of their chosen university.

For the documented TU BSW curriculum, applicants with +2, PCL or an equivalent qualification recognised by Tribhuvan University are eligible to apply.

An entrance examination is required under that curriculum. Admission is based on the qualifying examination, merit list and available seats.

Other universities may apply different academic requirements. For example, Kadambari Memorial College's Purbanchal University-affiliated programme specifies 12 years of schooling, a minimum of 45% aggregate or a CGPA of 2.0, and at least D+ in each subject.

Students should check the latest admission notice of their preferred college before applying.

## BSW Admission Process

The admission process may vary by university and college. Generally, students can follow these steps:

1. Check the admission notice and eligibility requirements.
2. Select a university-affiliated or constituent college offering BSW.
3. Complete the application process and submit the required documents.
4. Appear in the entrance examination or selection process, if required.
5. Complete admission formalities after selection.

## BSW Fees in Nepal

The cost of studying BSW varies according to the university, college, facilities and academic structure.

Some colleges publish their own fee structures, while others provide the details directly through their admission offices. Students should request a breakdown of admission fees, tuition fees, examination charges and other applicable costs.

Scholarships and financial support may also be available depending on the institution.

There is no single fixed fee applicable to every BSW college in Nepal.

## BSW vs BA in Social Work

BSW and BA in Social Work are closely related academic pathways. Their names and structures can differ depending on the university and curriculum.

At Tribhuvan University, the programme has been documented under both Bachelor in Social Work and Bachelor of Arts in Social Work (BASW) curriculum titles. Students should compare the exact curriculum, degree title and current programme status rather than assuming the names represent identical structures.
