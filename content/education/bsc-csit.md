## **What Is BSc CSIT?**

BSc CSIT stands for Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Information Technology. It is an undergraduate degree that combines the theoretical foundations of computer science with practical information technology.

The curriculum covers areas including:

- Programming
- Computer systems
- Digital logic
- Mathematics
- Data structures and algorithms
- Object-oriented programming
- Databases
- Computer networks
- Operating systems
- Software engineering
- Artificial intelligence
- Web-related technologies
- Data and information systems
- Project work
- Elective subjects
- Internship

The TU curriculum describes the program as covering the design and implementation of computer software systems, theoretical foundations of computer science and functional knowledge of computer hardware.

### **Is BSc CSIT a computer science or IT degree?**

BSc CSIT is both. The name itself reflects the combination: computer science **+ information technology.** The course therefore includes theoretical computer science as well as applied IT topics.

## **Why Study BSc CSIT in Nepal?**

Students choose BSc CSIT for different reasons. Some want to become software developers. Others are interested in databases, networking, cybersecurity, data, AI, systems or further study.

The curriculum gives students exposure to multiple computing areas rather than restricting them to one specialisation from the beginning.

A BSc CSIT student can develop foundations in:

- Programming
- Algorithms
- Software development
- Database systems
- Networking
- Computer systems
- Information technology
- Mathematics
- Artificial intelligence
- Project development

However, the degree itself does not guarantee a particular job.

A student's practical outcome also depends on:

- Programming practice
- Projects
- Internship experience
- Communication
- Problem-solving
- Portfolio
- Specialization
- Work experience
- Continuous learning

## **BSc CSIT Eligibility in Nepal**

The eligibility requirements should always be checked against the current [TU/IOST](https://iost.tu.edu.np/) admission notice, because admission rules and notices can change. The TU curriculum states that applicants should have completed 12 years of schooling in the science stream or an equivalent qualification, meet the stated academic requirement and pass the entrance examination conducted by IOST.

### **Who can study BSc CSIT?**

The traditional eligibility route is a **+2 Science or equivalent qualification** that satisfies TU's current requirements.

Students should verify:

- Academic background
- Required subjects
- Minimum grades/division
- Equivalent qualification rules
- Entrance requirement
- Application requirements

### **Can management students study BSc CSIT?**

BSc CSIT should **not be treated like [BCA](https://dikshangautam.com.np/education/academic-courses/bca/)**, where eligibility can be broader depending on the university.

The TU BSc CSIT curriculum specifies a science stream or equivalent background. Students from non-science backgrounds should therefore check the current IOST eligibility notice rather than assuming they qualify.

## **BSc CSIT Entrance Exam in Nepal**

Admission to BSc CSIT under TU involves the IOST entrance examination. The current IOST website is actively publishing BSc CSIT entrance information for the 2083 intake, including:

- Entrance examination notices
- Entrance results
- College priority order
- Admission lists
- Admission-related notices

For example, IOST published a **BSc CSIT Entrance Result 2083** notice on August 9, 2026, followed by college priority and admission information.

### **How does BSc CSIT admission generally work?**

The process can be summarised as:

**Check eligibility → Apply for entrance → Sit the IOST entrance examination → Receive result/rank → Follow the college priority/admission process → Complete college admission.**

Exact dates and procedures should be taken from the current IOST notice for the relevant intake.

### **What should you study for the entrance?**

Preparation should be based on the **official entrance syllabus and current notice**, not an old preparation guide.

Students commonly need strong foundations in areas relevant to the entrance examination, including:

- Mathematics
- Physics
- English
- Computer-related fundamentals

But the exact examination structure should be verified against the current official notice/syllabus before preparation.

## **BSc CSIT Duration**

BSc CSIT is a four-year undergraduate program divided into eight semesters. TU's curriculum specifies eight semesters and 126 credit hours. That means students normally progress through:

**Year 1 → Year 2 → Year 3 → Year 4**

with two semesters in each academic year.

### **Does BSc CSIT have mathematics?**

Yes, mathematics is part of the BSc CSIT curriculum, including Mathematics I and Mathematics II, along with statistics and discrete structures.

This is one of the important differences students should consider when comparing BSc CSIT with other computing degrees.

## **BSc CSIT Subjects: What Will You Actually Learn?**

## Instead of thinking about the syllabus only as a list of subjects, it is more useful to divide it into skill areas.

### **1. Programming**

You build foundations in programming and progress toward more advanced programming concepts.

### **2. Computer Science**

You study concepts such as:

- Algorithms
- Data structures
- Operating systems
- Computer architecture
- Artificial intelligence
- Computational concepts

### **3. Information Technology**

You encounter:

- Databases
- Networking
- Web technologies
- Information systems
- Software engineering

### **4. Mathematics and Statistics**

Mathematical subjects provide the analytical foundation used across computer science.

### **5. Practical Work**

Projects and internship components give students opportunities to apply academic knowledge.

## **BSc CSIT Fees in Nepal**

There is no single BSc CSIT course fee for every college in Nepal. This is an important point. BSc CSIT course in Nepal is offered through TU constituent and affiliated institutions, and the total cost can differ substantially between institutions.

Current third-party listings show substantial variation between public/constituent campuses and private colleges. For example, current 2026 listings report constituent-college fees in the lower range and private-college fees considerably higher, but these figures vary by institution and should not be treated as a national fixed fee.

### **Why are BSc CSIT fees different between colleges?**

### Costs can vary because of:

- Institution type
- Facilities
- Location
- College-specific fee structure
- Laboratory resources
- Additional services
- Scholarship arrangements

Always verify the current fee directly with the college before admission.

## **BSc CSIT Colleges in Nepal**

### **BSc CSIT Frequently Asked Questions**

**What is BSc CSIT in Nepal?**

BSc CSIT is the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Information Technology under Tribhuvan University's Institute of Science and Technology. It is a four-year, eight-semester undergraduate program with 126 credit hours.

### **How many years is BSc CSIT?**

BSc CSIT takes **four academic years**, divided into **eight semesters**.

### **How many credit hours is BSc CSIT?**

The TU curriculum specifies **126 credit hours**.

### **Who can study BSc CSIT?**

The TU curriculum specifies a science-stream or equivalent background and requires students to satisfy the entrance and application requirements. Always check the latest IOST notice for the current intake.

### **Is there an entrance exam for BSc CSIT?**

Yes. BSc CSIT admission under TU involves the entrance examination conducted through IOST.

### **Is BSc CSIT difficult?**

It can be challenging because it combines programming, mathematics, computer science, systems and practical work. Consistent practice makes a major difference.

### **Does BSc CSIT include mathematics?**

Yes. Mathematics and statistics are included in the curriculum.

### **Does BSc CSIT include programming?**

Yes. Programming is a major part of the curriculum, beginning with C programming and progressing through additional programming and computing subjects.
