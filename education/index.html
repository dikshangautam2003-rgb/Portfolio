Frequently asked

Education in Nepal: FAQs

Quick answers to the questions that often come before a bigger education decision.

What should I consider when choosing a college in Nepal?

Look beyond reputation and compare factors such as the program, affiliation, location, total cost, admission requirements and how well the college fits your priorities.

How do I choose the right course after +2?

Start by understanding the subjects and type of work that interest you, then compare the actual courses, admission requirements, costs and colleges offering them.

What should I check before college admission?

Confirm the program, affiliation, admission requirements, total fees, required documents, deadlines and any conditions that could affect your enrollment.

How should I compare college fees?

Don't compare only the advertised tuition figure. Look at the total cost and check what is included, along with other recurring or one-time expenses.

Should I use an education consultancy?

A consultancy may help with parts of the study-abroad process, but you should understand its services, fees and recommendations and verify important information independently.

Which countries can I explore for studying abroad?

Current study-abroad guides cover Australia, Canada, Japan and the UK, with destination-specific information to help you start comparing.
