BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

What should you compare?

There is no single factor that tells you whether a college, course or study option is right for you. Start with the things that matter most to your situation.

FactorQuestion to ask
CourseDoes the course actually match what you want to study?
CollegeDoes the college offer the course, environment and location you are looking for?
CostWhat costs will you need to plan for before committing?
AdmissionWhat are the eligibility, application process and important requirements?
LocationHow practical is the place where you will study?
Future plansHow does the course fit into what you want to do after graduation?

Education in Nepal: FAQs

What can I find on Dikshan's Education section?

You can explore college guides, courses and admissions information, study-abroad destinations and education consultancy guides.

Where can I find college guides in Nepal?

Start with the Colleges section for guides covering specific courses and locations.

What courses does Dikshan currently cover?

The current Education section includes course guides such as BCA and BSc CSIT, with more content organized under Courses & Admissions.

Does Dikshan provide study-abroad counselling?

Dikshan provides information and guides for researching study-abroad options. It is not presented as an education consultancy.
