Questions students ask

Education in Nepal: FAQs

What can I find on Dikshan's Education section?

You can explore college guides, courses and admissions information, study-abroad destinations and education consultancy guides.

Where can I find college guides in Nepal?

Start with the Colleges section, where you can explore guides for specific courses and locations.

What courses does Dikshan currently cover?

The current Education section includes guides such as BCA and BSc CSIT, with more course content organized under Courses & Admissions.

Does Dikshan provide study-abroad counselling?

Dikshan provides information and guides for researching study-abroad options. It is not presented as an education consultancy.

Which study-abroad destinations does Dikshan cover?

The current site structure includes Australia, Canada, Japan and the UK.

How should I choose a college in Nepal?

Start by comparing the course, college, location, cost, admission requirements and how well the option matches your plans.
