Core guide

What to think about before you decide.

Use these principles to compare options without letting marketing or one metric make the decision for you.

01How to compare a Bachelor's degree

Look at curriculum, teaching model, practical work, internship opportunities, total cost, location, entrance requirements and how the degree fits the role you want to grow toward.

02Use official program pages as your source of truth

Program names, eligibility, fees and intake dates can change. Start with the university or campus notice, then use other reviews and student experiences to add context.

03Make the first semester a research sprint

Use early semesters to test subjects, build practical work, join projects and discover which skills deserve deeper investment beyond the classroom.
