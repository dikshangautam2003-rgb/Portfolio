+2 / NEB: Choosing a Stream and Faculty

A practical starting point for students entering +2 under the NEB system.

Popular streams

  • Science (Physical / Biology)
  • Management
  • Humanities & Social Sciences
  • Education
  • Law

How to choose

Start with what you enjoy studying day-to-day, not just the career it's supposed to lead to — most streams keep more doors open than students assume. Talk to current +2 students in the stream you're considering, not just prospectus copy.

What to check before enrolling

  • Board affiliation and exam pattern
  • Faculty availability for the subjects you actually want
  • Practical/lab facilities if choosing Science
  • Fee structure and what it includes

After +2

Your +2 stream narrows but doesn't lock your bachelor's options. See Bachelor's for how common degree paths connect back to +2 streams.
