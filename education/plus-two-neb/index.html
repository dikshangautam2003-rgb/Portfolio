Core guide

What to think about before you decide.

Use these principles to compare options without letting marketing or one metric make the decision for you.

01Choose a stream by fit, not prestige

Compare the kind of subjects and learning work you enjoy with the degree paths you may want later. The strongest choice is usually the one that keeps realistic options open while matching how you learn.

02Build a repeatable NEB study week

Use a simple cycle: class notes → same-week revision → problem practice → weekly review. The goal is consistency, not a perfect timetable.

03Inspect a college before enrolling

Ask about class size, practical facilities, teacher availability, internal assessment, transport, total fees and the actual timetable rather than judging the campus by advertising alone.
