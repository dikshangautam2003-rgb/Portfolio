Best BCA Colleges in Kathmandu — What to Compare Before You Choose

There is no single BCA college that is best for every student. A better shortlist starts with the awarding university, location, budget and the parts of student life that matter to you.

Updated September 2026Dikshan guide
Photo: Vitaly Gariev / Unsplash
The short answer

There is no single BCA college that is best for every student. A better shortlist starts with the awarding university, location, budget and the parts of student life that matter to you.

Start with the awarding university

BCA is not one identical national programme. Different universities can have different curricula, entrance processes and academic rules, so affiliation should be one of the first filters in your shortlist.

Then compare the college

Once the degree and university fit, compare the things that can vary from one college to another: location, classroom environment, labs, faculty access, student activities, internships and the overall cost of attendance.

Questions worth asking before admission

Ask for the current fee structure, what is included, how practical classes are run, how internships are arranged, what recent students did after graduation, and which facilities are actually available to BCA students.

What to verify before acting

Prices, admission rules, availability and other time-sensitive details can change. Use the linked sources and the latest official information before making a final decision.
