Study Abroad Roadmap for Nepali Students

A calm, practical guide to planning international study in the USA, Australia, UK, Canada, and Europe without agency confusion or surprise expenses.

Consumer Caution

An educational consultancy is a service provider, not a visa officer. Never pay for "guaranteed visa" promises or submit fake financial documents that can result in 10-year immigration bans.

Strategic Milestones

Three Foundational Guides for International Study

Actionable timelines, financial preparation methods, and questions to ask educational consultancies in Kathmandu.

GUIDE 01 · 12-MONTH TIMELINE

The 12-Month Preparation Roadmap for Nepali Students

↓

Planning international study requires at least 9 to 12 months of structured preparation before your target intake (Fall/August or Spring/January).

Month-by-Month Milestone Checklist

  • Months 12–10: Test Preparation: Prepare for and sit for English language proficiency tests (IELTS Academic or PTE Academic). Target at least 6.5 overall (no band less than 6.0) or PTE 58+. If targeting US undergraduate scholarships, prepare for the SAT.
  • Months 9–7: University Research & Shortlisting: Select 6–8 universities categorized into Dream, Target, and Safety tiers based on your GPA, test scores, and total annual budget.
  • Months 6–5: Application Submission: Finalize your Statement of Purpose (SOP), secure academic recommendation letters from your +2 or college teachers, order official transcripts, and submit applications.
  • Months 4–3: Offer Letters & Financial Documentation: Review conditional vs. unconditional offers. Prepare bank balance certificates, tax clearance records, property valuations, and relation verifications.
  • Months 2–1: Visa Lodgement & MOEST NOC: Pay initial tuition deposit (or obtain I-20 / CoE). Apply online for the MOEST No Objection Certificate (noc.moest.gov.np) and lodge your student visa application.
GUIDE 02 · DUE DILIGENCE

How to Choose an Educational Consultancy in Kathmandu

↓

There are over a thousand consultancies operating across Bagbazar, Putalisadak, and New Baneshwor. Distinguish ethical counselors from commission-driven sales brokers.

Red Flags to Avoid

  • Pushing Unknown Regional Colleges: If a counselor pushes you relentlessly toward a specific private institute you have never heard of, they are likely receiving an unusually high recruitment commission.
  • "100% Visa Guarantee": No agency controls embassy visa officers. Such claims are illegal under Ministry regulations.
  • Withholding Original Documents: Never hand over your original passport or academic certificates to an agency for "safekeeping".
  • Fake Financials Suggestion: Never agree to fabricate bank balances or income sources. Embassies cross-verify financial paperwork directly with Nepali commercial banks.
GUIDE 03 · COST ESTIMATION

The Real Cost of Studying Abroad: Beyond First Semester Tuition

↓

Many Nepali families only budget for the initial deposit and visa fee, failing to account for true living costs, health insurance, and work restriction laws.

True Budget Line Items

  • Pre-Departure Expenses: Test fees, application fees, SEVIS/visa fees, biometric appointments, medical examinations, and flight tickets (~NPR 3–5 Lakhs).
  • Tuition Deposit: First semester or first year tuition (~USD $8,000–$25,000 depending on country and institution).
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC): Mandatory health insurance for the full visa duration.
  • Arrival Buffer: At least 2–3 months of living expenses in cash/bank card to cover accommodation bond, food, and transport before securing part-time work.
