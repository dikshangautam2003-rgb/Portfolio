Core guide

What to think about before you decide.

Use these principles to compare options without letting marketing or one metric make the decision for you.

01Build the shortlist before the paperwork

Start with subject, degree level, budget, location and realistic academic fit. A clear shortlist makes every later application decision easier.

02Ask better questions about money

Separate tuition, living costs, insurance, application fees, travel and other expenses. Ask the institution or consultancy to explain every required payment clearly.

03Choose a consultancy by its process, not its promise

Ask who handles your application, what is included, which documents you must verify yourself and what happens if the university or visa outcome differs from the plan.
