Study Abroad: Where to Start

A realistic overview of the study-abroad process for Nepali students — Australia, UK, Canada and Japan.

Popular destinations

  • Australia
  • United Kingdom
  • Canada
  • Japan

How to compare destinations

Compare on total cost of living plus tuition (not tuition alone), post-study work rights, and how realistic the visa process is for your specific profile — not just university rankings.

The general process

  1. Shortlist universities and programs based on your actual academic background
  2. Sit any required English tests (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL depending on destination)
  3. Apply and secure an offer letter
  4. Arrange financial documentation
  5. Apply for the student visa

Working with a consultancy

A good consultancy explains trade-offs and fees upfront and never pressures a decision. Ask directly how they're compensated (commission from universities is common and not inherently a problem, but you should know about it).
