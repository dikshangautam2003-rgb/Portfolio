What to look at first

Start with the constraints that can eliminate options quickly: location, budget, timing, eligibility or the kind of experience you want. Once those are clear, compare the remaining options on the details that matter to you.

Questions worth answering

  • What is the current cost or price?
  • What is included, and what is not?
  • What changes between the options?
  • Which option is a poor fit for a particular type of user?
  • Which details need to be verified directly before you commit?
Keep time-sensitive details current

Fees, prices, admission windows, travel rules and availability can change. Use the latest official information before acting.
