Core guide

What to think about before you decide.

Use these principles to compare options without letting marketing or one metric make the decision for you.

01Map the funding before the deadline

Create a simple table of scholarship name, provider, eligibility, required documents, deadline and what the award actually covers.

02Write for evidence and fit

Show academic work, leadership, community involvement or other relevant evidence that connects directly to the scholarship criteria.

03Read the renewal conditions

Funding may have academic or administrative conditions. Understand them before treating a scholarship as guaranteed for the full degree.
