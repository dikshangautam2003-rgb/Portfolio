Scholarships: A Realistic Overview

What scholarships in Nepal and abroad actually look like, and how to avoid wasting time on the wrong ones.

Types of scholarships

  • Government scholarships (need- or merit-based)
  • University-specific merit scholarships
  • Destination-country scholarships for international students
  • Private/NGO-funded scholarships

How to prioritise your search

Start with scholarships tied directly to the university and program you're already applying to — they usually have the best odds relative to effort, compared with broad, highly competitive open scholarships.

What to prepare in advance

  • Academic transcripts and certificates, translated if required
  • A clear, honest personal statement — not a generic template
  • Recommendation letters requested well ahead of deadlines

Red flags

Any "scholarship" that asks for an upfront processing fee before you've even been considered deserves scrutiny — legitimate scholarships are funded by the granting body, not the applicant.
