Core guide

What to think about before you decide.

Use these principles to compare options without letting marketing or one metric make the decision for you.

01Write the SOP around evidence

Explain what you studied, what you did with it, what changed your interests and why the next program makes sense. Specific evidence is more useful than polished clichés.

02Build an admissions calendar

Put entrance exams, applications, document requests, offer conditions, financial planning and deadlines into one visible timeline.

03Read every condition before paying

An offer can include academic, document, financial or intake conditions. Keep a written checklist of what must happen next.
