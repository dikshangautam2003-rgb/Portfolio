How Admissions Actually Work

What actually happens between applying and enrolling, and what to check at each stage.

The general steps

  1. Shortlist programs against your interests and eligibility
  2. Submit application with required documents
  3. Entrance exam or interview, where applicable
  4. Offer/admission letter
  5. Fee payment and enrollment

Questions worth asking before enrolling

  • What exactly does the quoted fee include?
  • What's the refund policy if you change your mind after paying?
  • What's the actual class size and faculty-to-student ratio?

Red flags

  • Pressure to pay immediately to "lock in a seat"
  • Vague answers about faculty qualifications
  • No willingness to let you speak with current students
