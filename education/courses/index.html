How to choose practical short courses in web development, digital marketing, data, and design without paying for superficial certificates.

Portfolio Over Paper

Three Evaluation Frameworks for Course Learners

Learn how to select the right training institute, decide between online self-study and classroom attendance, and decode a syllabus.

GUIDE 01 · DUE DILIGENCE

A Better Way to Choose a Short Course in Kathmandu

↓

Scores of training institutes in Putalisadak, Bagbazar, and Tinkune promise "100% Guaranteed Job Placement" in 3 months. In reality, real employment is earned through demonstrable skills.

Questions to Ask Any Institute Before Paying Tuition

  • Who is the Instructor? Are they an active industry practitioner with a public portfolio, or a recent graduate teaching from slides?
  • Project Capstone Requirement: Will you build a real-world, end-to-end project of your own creation, or simply copy-paste a tutorial clone that 200 other students have in their CV?
  • Class Size Limit: Is the batch capped at 12–15 students to allow individual screen-sharing and code reviews, or are there 40 students sharing two monitors?
  • Post-Course Mentorship: Does the institute offer code audits, portfolio reviews, and resume polishing after the lecture weeks conclude?
GUIDE 02 · LEARNING FORMAT

Self-Paced Online vs. Classroom Bootcamp: What Fits Your Week?

↓

With thousands of free and low-cost courses available globally (freeCodeCamp, The Odin Project, Coursera, YouTube), you may not even need an in-person institute.

Comparison Matrix

  • Choose In-Person Classroom if: You struggle with self-motivation, need fixed accountability, prefer in-person peer collaboration, and benefit from immediate hand-raising for bug fixing.
  • Choose Online Self-Paced if: You are disciplined, have a reliable internet connection at home, want to learn at your own speed, and prefer building independent projects rather than following a rigid group timeline.
  • The Hybrid Strategy (Recommended): Use free online curricula for the core theory, build projects on your own, and pay for 1-on-1 mentor code reviews only when you get stuck.
GUIDE 03 · CURRICULUM AUDIT

How to Read a Course Outline Like a Senior Engineer

↓

Institutes often cram 40 buzzwords into a 2-month syllabus to look impressive. Look for foundational depth over superficial breadth.

Green Flags vs. Red Flags

  • Green Flag: The outline allocates substantial time to Version Control (Git/GitHub), basic debugging, data structures, semantic HTML/CSS, and automated testing.
  • Red Flag: The outline claims to teach 6 different programming languages and 4 frameworks in 8 weeks (e.g. "Master HTML, CSS, JS, React, Node, Python, Django and AWS in 60 days"). You will learn none of them deeply.
  • Crucial Checkpoint: Ensure the course teaches how to solve errors using documentation and terminal tools, rather than memorizing syntax.
