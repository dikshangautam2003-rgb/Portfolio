Short Courses & Certifications

Where short courses and certifications fit alongside a degree — and when they're worth it.

When a course helps

Short courses are most useful when they fill a specific, demonstrable gap: a portfolio piece, a tool you need for a job you're applying to, or a skill your degree program doesn't cover in enough depth.

When to be skeptical

  • Certificates with no practical project or portfolio output
  • Courses that promise guaranteed job placement
  • Anything priced far above the market rate for the same syllabus elsewhere

How to evaluate one

Ask to see the actual syllabus, who teaches it, and what graduates have gone on to build or do — not just the marketing page.
