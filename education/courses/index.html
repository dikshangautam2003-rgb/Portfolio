Core guide

What to think about before you decide.

Use these principles to compare options without letting marketing or one metric make the decision for you.

01Read the syllabus before buying

Look for fundamentals, practical assignments, feedback, project work and a realistic progression. A long list of buzzwords is not the same as a deep curriculum.

02Choose delivery around your actual week

Classroom learning can add structure and accountability; self-paced learning can add flexibility. The useful option is the one you can consistently practice.

03Measure the skill, not the certificate

Before enrolling, ask what you should be able to build, write, analyse, design or explain by the end of the course.
