How Much Does a Business Website Actually Cost in Nepal?

Dikshan GautamPractical guide

There is no single price for a business website in Nepal. The useful question is what the website needs to do, how much content it needs, and how much work sits behind the design.

1. A brochure site is not the same as a growth site

A simple site with five or six pages has a very different scope from a site that needs SEO architecture, content, integrations, analytics and ongoing publishing.

2. Design is only one part of the cost

Research, information architecture, copy, development, CMS setup, SEO, forms, analytics, testing and content migration can all be part of a serious business website.

3. Budget around the business problem

If the goal is simply credibility, keep the scope lean. If the website is expected to generate leads from search, invest more heavily in structure, content and conversion paths.

4. Ask what happens after launch

A website needs maintenance, content updates, analytics and occasional technical work. A cheap build can become expensive if nobody owns those jobs later.

5. Prioritise the pages that matter

Start with the homepage, service pages, about, contact and the core content that answers customer questions. Expand once the foundation works.
