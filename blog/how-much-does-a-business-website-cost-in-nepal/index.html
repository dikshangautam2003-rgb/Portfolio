How Much Does a Business Website Actually Cost in Nepal?

Dikshan Gautam8 min read

"How much does a website cost?" is a bit like asking "how much does a house cost?" — it depends on what you actually need. Here's a more useful way to think about it than chasing a single number.

What actually drives the price

How many genuinely different pages/templates the site needs, whether it needs custom functionality (booking, payments, multi-language), and how much original content needs to be created versus supplied.

A simple, informational site

A clear homepage, a few service or product pages, an about page and a contact page — built on solid structure with on-page SEO in place — is the right starting point for most small and mid-sized businesses.

Where budget is genuinely worth spending

  • Clear information architecture around what customers are actually trying to do
  • On-page SEO from day one, not added later
  • Mobile performance — most traffic in Nepal is mobile-first
  • A clear path to contact or enquiry on every page
Custom animations, unnecessary "just in case" functionality, and template complexity that doesn't match how customers actually use the site are the most common places budget disappears without adding value.

What to ask before you get a quote

  • How many pages does the business actually need, realistically?
  • Will SEO structure be built in, or added afterward?
  • Who's responsible for content — copy and images — and is that included?
  • What happens after launch — is there a plan for updates?

The right question isn't "what's the cheapest website I can get" — it's "what does this business actually need to turn visitors into enquiries, and nothing more."
