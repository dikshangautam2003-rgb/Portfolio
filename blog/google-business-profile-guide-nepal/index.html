Google Business Profile: A Practical Guide

Dikshan GautamPractical guide

For a local business, the Google Business Profile is often the first digital impression a potential customer gets. Treat it like a storefront, not a form you fill once.

1. Claim the profile

Use the correct business name and complete verification. Do not create duplicate listings simply because you want to target more keywords.

2. Get the core information right

Address or service area, phone, website, opening hours and business category should be accurate and consistent.

3. Write for customers

Use the description and service fields to explain what you actually offer. Avoid stuffing city names or repeating keywords unnaturally.

4. Add real photos

Use current photographs of the business, team, location, products or work. Real visual evidence is more useful than generic stock imagery.

5. Build a review habit

Ask genuine customers for reviews, respond to them and use recurring feedback to understand what customers value most.
