QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU BUY

Buying products in Nepal: FAQs

How should I choose a product in Nepal?

Start by identifying what you need, your budget and the features that actually matter for your use. Then compare relevant products rather than choosing based only on specifications or brand.

What should I compare when buying electronics?

Price, important specifications, compatibility, warranty, service, seller, return conditions and practical limitations can all affect the decision.

Is the cheapest product always the best value?

Not necessarily. A cheaper option can make sense when its limitations don’t matter to you, while spending more may be worthwhile when it solves an important problem.

How should I compare product prices in Nepal?

Check whether you’re comparing the same model, variant and included accessories, and consider differences in seller, warranty, promotions and other purchase conditions.

Should I trust product specifications?

Specifications are useful, but they don’t always tell you how important a feature is in actual use. A good buying decision considers both the specification and whether it matters for your needs.
