BEFORE YOU BUY

Price is only one part of the decision.

Local price, warranty, service, availability and compatibility can change whether a product makes sense for you.

FactorWhat to check
PriceCompare the local price with the features you actually need.
Warranty & serviceKnow what support is available after purchase.
AvailabilityCheck whether the exact model and variant is realistically available.
CompatibilityMake sure the product works with your existing setup.
Seller & returnsUnderstand who you are buying from and what happens if something goes wrong.
Value over timeThink about durability, maintenance and how long you expect to use it.
