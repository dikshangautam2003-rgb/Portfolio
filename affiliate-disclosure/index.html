Affiliate Disclosure — Dikshan

How affiliate links and other commercial relationships can support Dikshan without determining editorial verdicts.

How commercial relationships fit

Dikshan may eventually use affiliate links, sponsored or featured placements, advertising, lead generation and other appropriate commercial models.

These relationships can help fund useful research. They should not silently turn a paid option into “the best” option.

Simple rule: money can influence what we cover; it cannot decide what we conclude.

Transparency

Where a commercial relationship could reasonably affect how a reader interprets a recommendation, it should be disclosed clearly near the relevant content.
