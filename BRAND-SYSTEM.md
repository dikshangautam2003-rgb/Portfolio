# Dikshan Brand System

## Brand position
Dikshan is a Nepal-focused research and recommendation platform helping people make better decisions through independently researched, evidence-backed recommendations.

**Brand idea:** Deep research underneath. Simple, honest advice on the surface.

## Public navigation
Education · Products · Travel · Research · About · Search

Services and Work are no longer primary navigation. Commercial work can remain secondary and contextual.

## Logo system
The primary mark is a compact **D** monogram built from the Dikshan wordmark geometry. It is designed to work independently as the favicon, social avatar and small UI mark.

- Favicon / mark: `/assets/dikshan-mark.svg`
- Full wordmark: `/assets/dikshan-logo.svg`
- Primary mark background: Ink `#17202A`
- Mark foreground: Paper `#FAF8F3`
- Accent slash: Vermilion `#C74634`

Keep the mark visually simple at small sizes. Do not add shadows, gradients or extra decorative elements. The favicon and full wordmark must always use the same geometry and color system.

## Visual character
Editorial, intelligent, grounded, modern, distinctly Nepal-rooted without cliché tourism styling. The interface should feel calm and trustworthy, with strong typographic hierarchy and restrained use of color.

## Color tokens
- Ink: #17202A
- Himalayan Blue: #1F5A7A
- Vermilion: #C74634
- Saffron: #D99A2B
- Paper: #FAF8F3
- White: #FFFFFF
- Stone: #E5E0D7
- Muted Text: #5F6870
- Soft Blue: #EEF4F6
- Soft Warm: #F4EFE6

Use Ink for primary text and navigation. Himalayan Blue is the main brand/action color. Vermilion is reserved for decisive emphasis, badges, warnings and selected CTAs. Saffron is an accent, not a dominant brand color. Avoid large gradients and decorative color overload.

## Typography
Primary UI/body: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
Display fallback: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif, used sparingly for editorial moments if native fallback is necessary.

Recommended sizing:
- Body: 18px / 1.7
- Small/meta: 13–14px / 1.5
- H1 desktop: clamp(3.2rem, 6vw, 5.2rem), line-height 0.98–1.05
- H2 desktop: clamp(2.2rem, 4vw, 3.3rem), line-height 1.05–1.12
- H3: 1.25–1.6rem
- Buttons: 14px, 700–800 weight

Keep reading columns around 60–75 characters per line. Do not use tiny body text.

## UI language
- Fine 1px borders instead of heavy shadows.
- 10–16px corner radius for normal UI; 18–24px only for feature cards/media.
- Plenty of whitespace.
- Strong left alignment and editorial rhythm.
- Visible keyboard focus states.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Do not use decorative animation that competes with reading.

## Imagery
Prefer original/local imagery, real products, real places, researchers/writers, and documentary-style photography. Avoid generic business stock imagery. Every meaningful image needs useful alt text; decorative imagery gets empty alt text.

The article system now supports a 16:9 lead image, evidence-note imagery, and image-led related-content cards. Remote image URLs are used only in the internal template preview; published articles should replace them with owned/optimized local or properly licensed assets wherever possible.

## Editorial UX
Important recommendations should surface their verdict quickly, followed by why, evidence confidence, limitations, and methodology. Trust information should be visible without overwhelming the page.

## Brand voice
Direct, human, informed, calm, honest. Avoid hype, empty superlatives and corporate filler.

## Core principle
Search engines bring the person. Research earns the trust.
