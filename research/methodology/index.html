Methodology

How Dikshan turns information into recommendations.

The same evidence is not equally useful for every claim. Our method starts by asking what we need to know, then finds the sources most capable of answering it.

Evidence hierarchy

Primary → independent → community → weak signals.

Primary/direct sources include government, regulatory, university, manufacturer, original datasets, direct interviews and first-hand testing. Strong independent evidence includes reputable journalism and independent testing. Customer/community evidence is valuable for lived experience. Weak or unverified sources are mainly leads for further investigation.

Evidence strength depends on the claim, not simply the source.

Confidence

High. Moderate. Limited. Not enough evidence.

We don't turn missing information into a negative score. If the evidence isn't sufficient to support a strong recommendation, the correct editorial outcome can be “not enough evidence.”

Research dates and meaningful updates are recorded so readers can judge freshness.
