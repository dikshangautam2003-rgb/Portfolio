Dikshan · Editorial

Dikshan methodology

Methodology supports the product; it does not replace it. The goal is to make the reasoning behind important recommendations understandable and consistent.

What matters most

Pages should be useful, specific and honest about what is known, what is uncertain and what a reader should verify before acting.

Evidence in practice

The source depends on the claim. Official information is appropriate for official fees and rules; independent testing is useful for real-world performance; repeated user experiences can reveal patterns that official pages do not show.

Editorial independence

Commercial relationships can affect what gets covered, but they should not decide what a guide concludes.
