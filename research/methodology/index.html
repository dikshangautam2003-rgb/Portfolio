Methodology — Evidence Before Confidence

A public framework for evaluating evidence, freshness, testing, recommendations and editorial independence.

Evidence hierarchy

EvidenceTypical role
Primary sourcesOfficial facts, requirements, specifications, pricing, regulations and institutional information.
Independent sourcesReputable journalism, established publications, independent testing and research.
Community evidenceCustomer experiences, forums, marketplaces and social platforms used as signals and context.
Weak sourcesUnsourced claims, copied listicles and anonymous statements should not be treated as strong evidence.

Recommendation standard

Recommendations should connect evidence to a specific user need. A universal winner is often less useful than a clear “best for” verdict with an important limitation.

Editorial safeguard: commercial relationships can influence what Dikshan covers, but they cannot dictate what Dikshan concludes.

What we will not claim

We will not invent testing, visits, interviews, user reviews, rankings, prices or performance results. Where evidence is incomplete, the page should make that limitation visible.
