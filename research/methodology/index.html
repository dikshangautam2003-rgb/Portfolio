Research · Ranking methodology

Research tells us what is true. Criteria tell us what matters.

There is no universal score that makes a college, washing machine and hotel comparable. Category-specific criteria turn evidence into a decision.

Education

Recognition & fit

Affiliation, eligibility, fees, curriculum, environment and outcomes where reliable evidence exists.

Products

Performance & value

Price, performance, reliability signals, warranty, service, availability and user fit.

Travel

Experience & logistics

Difficulty, duration, season, crowds, access, cost, location and experience.

Internal assessment

The Dikshan Score stays internal.

We may use structured numerical assessment internally to keep comparisons consistent. Readers see the editorial verdict, best-for guidance, evidence confidence, limitations and reasoning — not false mathematical certainty.

A category can be left unranked when evidence does not support a responsible conclusion.
