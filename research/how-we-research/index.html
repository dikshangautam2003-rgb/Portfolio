Research · How we work

We show our work.

Recommendations are built from evidence, not confidence. We make the evidence hierarchy, uncertainty and commercial boundaries visible.

01 · Evidence

Primary first. Context always.

Government, regulatory, university, manufacturer and other primary sources are preferred when they directly answer a claim. Independent testing and reputable publications add context. Customer and community experiences help us understand real-world patterns.

We do not treat five copied listicles as five independent sources.

02 · Reviews

A review is an experience, not a universal truth.

We look for specificity, reviewer history, consistency, recency and cross-platform patterns. Suspicious signals are treated cautiously rather than casually labelled fake.

Anecdotes are signals. Patterns become evidence.

03 · Confidence

Sometimes the right answer is “not enough evidence.”

Recommendations can carry evidence confidence: High, Moderate, Limited or Not enough evidence. Missing evidence is not automatically negative evidence.
