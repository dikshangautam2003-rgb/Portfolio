Research & methodology

We show our work.

Dikshan's recommendations are built from evidence, not confidence. Here's how we collect information, evaluate it and decide what we can responsibly publish.

01 · Evidence

Primary first. Context always.

Government, regulatory, university, manufacturer and other primary sources are preferred when they directly answer a claim. Independent testing and reputable publications add context. Customer and community experiences help us understand real-world patterns.

We don't treat five copied listicles as five independent sources. Repetition is not independence.

02 · Reviews

A review is an experience, not a universal truth.

We look for specificity, reviewer history, consistency, recency and cross-platform patterns. Suspicious or difficult-to-verify review signals are treated cautiously rather than casually labelled fake.

Anecdotes are signals. Patterns become evidence.

03 · Confidence

Sometimes the right answer is “not enough evidence.”

Every recommendation can carry an evidence confidence level: High, Moderate, Limited or Not enough evidence. Missing evidence is not automatically negative evidence.
