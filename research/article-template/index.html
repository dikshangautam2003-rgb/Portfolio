What the evidence says

Separate official facts, independent evidence and community signals. Match the source type to the claim: official fees should come from an official source; real-world reliability may require independent testing and repeated customer patterns.

Evidence note

Show one important research finding here, with a source link and date. Do not imply first-hand experience unless the team actually tested or visited the subject.

Primary sources

Use government, regulatory, university/college, manufacturer, original data, direct interviews or verified first-hand testing where relevant.

Independent evidence

Use reputable journalism, independent testing, established publications and research. Note conflicts or gaps rather than smoothing them over.

Customer and community evidence

Use reviews, Reddit, YouTube, marketplaces and forums as signals. Look for specificity, reviewer history, repeated patterns and cross-platform consistency. A single anecdote is not a verdict.
